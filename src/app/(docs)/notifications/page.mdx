# Generation notifications

A detailed model on the High quality setting can take **up to ten minutes**.
You are not meant to sit and watch it. Turn on a notification and close the
tab.

<Callout type="note">
  **Anything that runs longer than two minutes** gets a notification — nothing
  faster does, since it lands while you're still looking at it. In practice
  that means models: textures, item art and icons come back in five to twenty
  seconds. There's no per-generation choice to make and nothing to switch on
  per asset; the length of the run decides.
</Callout>

## Turning them on

All three live on your [account page](https://studio.resourcepack.ai/account),
under **Notifications**. They're independent — turn on as many as you like, or
none.

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Email" icon="image">
    Goes to the address on your account. Needs that address verified, which it
    already is if you signed up with a password.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Discord" icon="plug">
    A direct message from our bot. Needs Discord connected to your account,
    and you in our server — see below.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In game" icon="server">
    A line of chat while you're on <ServerAddress />, with a clickable
    link back to the model. Needs your Minecraft account linked.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Discord

Two things have to be true, and they're both one-offs:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Discord to your account">
    On your account page, under **Sign-in methods**, hit **Connect** next to
    Discord. That's the same connection you'd use to sign in with — there's
    no separate notification setup, and nothing to type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join our Discord">
    A bot can only message people it shares a server with. That's Discord's
    rule, not ours, and it means the connection above isn't enough on its own
    — [join here](https://resourcepack.ai/discord) once and the DM has
    somewhere to land.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Skip the second step and the switch will sit there looking on while no
  message ever arrives. If DMs aren't reaching you, that's the first thing to
  check — followed by **Privacy Settings → Direct Messages** in the server,
  which can also block them.
</Callout>

## In game

Requires a linked Minecraft account — the same one-command `/permlink` setup
described in [Our test server](/testing/test-server). The message arrives
wherever you're standing on <ServerAddress />.

If you're offline when the model finishes, nothing is queued for later: the
message is a tap on the shoulder, not an inbox. Turn on email as well if you
want one that waits for you.

## What you get told

Both outcomes, not just the good one — a model that failed after twenty
minutes is exactly the thing you need to hear about, and the message says so
and links you to the Retry button. Failed generations refund their credits
automatically; see [Packs and assets](/packs).

## Turning them off

Same card, same switches. Every email also carries a line at the bottom
saying where to find them. Disconnecting Discord under Sign-in methods stops
the DMs too, though the switch is the gentler way to do it.
