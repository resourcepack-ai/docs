# Generation notifications

A model on the Studio tier can take **40 minutes**. You are not meant to sit
and watch it. Turn on a notification and close the tab.

<Callout type="note">
  Only **models and animations** notify. Textures, item art and icons finish in
  five to twenty seconds — the toast is still on screen when they land, so
  pinging you about one would just teach you to mute the channel that matters.
</Callout>

## Turning them on

All three live on your [account page](https://studio.resourcepack.ai/account),
under **Notifications**. They're independent — turn on as many as you like, or
none.

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Email" icon="image">
    Goes to the address on your account. Needs that address verified, which it
    already is if you signed up with a password.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Discord" icon="plug">
    A direct message from our bot. Needs one command — see below.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In game" icon="server">
    A line of chat while you're on **play.resourcepack.ai**, with a clickable
    link back to the model. Needs your Minecraft account linked.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Discord

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Discord">
    Our bot can only message people it shares a server with — that's a Discord
    rule, not ours. [Join here](https://resourcepack.ai/discord) first, or the
    DM has nowhere to land.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a code">
    On your account page, hit **Get link code** in the Notifications card. It's
    eight digits and lasts ten minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run /link">
    Type `/link <code>` anywhere in the Discord. The reply is only visible to
    you, and the card in studio updates on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The bot's full command list, usable anywhere it can see you:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/link <code>` | Binds this Discord account to your studio account and turns DMs on |
| `/unlink` | Stops the DMs and forgets the link |
| `/status` | Shows which account you're linked to, and whether pings are on |

<Callout type="note">
  If you signed in to studio *with* Discord, we'll try that account as a
  fallback — but only `/link` guarantees the bot can actually reach you, so
  it's worth doing even then.
</Callout>

## In game

Requires a linked Minecraft account — the same one-command `/permlink` setup
described in [Our test server](/testing/test-server). The message arrives
wherever you're standing on **play.resourcepack.ai**.

If you're offline when the model finishes, nothing is queued for later: the
message is a tap on the shoulder, not an inbox. Turn on email as well if you
want one that waits for you.

## What you get told

Both outcomes, not just the good one — a model that failed after twenty
minutes is exactly the thing you need to hear about, and the message says so
and links you to the Retry button. Failed generations refund their credits
automatically; see [Packs and assets](/packs).

## Turning them off

Same card, same switches. Every email also carries a line at the bottom saying
where to find them, and `/unlink` in Discord stops the DMs immediately.
