# Bring your own key

Add your own OpenAI key, and **3D models (generating, editing and animating
them) bill to your OpenAI account instead of your AI Credits**. Everything else
still uses AI Credits.

It's on every paid plan. Starter doesn't include it.

## Set it up

1. Make an API key in your OpenAI account.
2. In Studio, open **Account** and find **Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)**.
3. Paste the key and save it.
4. Optionally, pick the **Orchestrator model** that runs on it, to balance
   price against quality.

From then on, 3D model actions cost 0 AI Credits and show up on your OpenAI
bill instead. To go back to AI Credits, remove the key.

For a Team, an admin sets the key in the Team's settings. One key covers the
whole Team, and Team packs always use the Team's key, never a member's own.
See [Teams](/teams).

## What your key actually pays for

Your key runs the **orchestration layer**: the language model that plans a 3D
model, decides what to build and checks the result. That's the only part of
the work that runs on a standard provider, so it's the only part a key can
cover.

The rest is ours. Every model goes through our own proprietary build
pipeline, which we run and pay for ourselves whether or not you bring a key.
So a key doesn't make a model free to make: we still carry most of the cost,
and the key takes over the part that can be handed over.

## Why only 3D models

Blocks, items, icons, GUIs and the rest of the picture work come from our own
image models. There's no orchestration step on a standard provider there, only
proprietary models we built and host, so there's no key that could stand in for
them. We'd like to offer the same for pictures, and if that changes, it will be
listed here.

## Your key is safe with us

Your key is encrypted before it's stored, and it's only read at the moment a
3D model action runs on it. If a key stops working (revoked, out of funds, or
your plan ends), Studio stops using it and those actions go back to AI Credits.
If your plan ends, a key you stored is kept but isn't used until you're on a
paid plan again.
