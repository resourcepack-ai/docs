# Distribution

Distribution is how you give your pack to **every player on your own
server**. You connect your server to a pack once. After that, whenever you
press **Publish** in Studio, everyone who joins gets the new version
automatically. Studio also shows you who downloaded it.

It's the step after testing. **Sync** (see [The plugin](/testing/plugin))
sends a pack to just you while you build it. Distribution is the finished pack
your server runs for everybody.

You need [RP Engine](/testing/plugin), our free plugin, on the server.

## Setting it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the pack">
    In Studio, open **Distribution** in the sidebar, press **Serve a pack**,
    and choose the pack your server should hand its players.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your server">
    You get an eight-digit code, good for 30 minutes. On your server, as an
    operator (or anyone with the `rpengine.distribute` permission), run:

    ```
    /rp distribute 12345678
    ```

    This is called **binding** the server to the pack. The server remembers
    it, so you do it once. A pack can be bound to up to 10 servers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Press **Publish**, tick the Minecraft versions your players use, and
    confirm. Each ticked version is built straight away, so nobody waits when
    they join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Players get the pack the next time they join. No restart is needed.

## Updating the pack

Change the pack in Studio as usual, then press **New release** on its
Distribution page. It builds your ticked versions again and replaces what
players get. There's one live release at a time, and no rollback.

Players only ever get what you last published, not what's in the editor, so a
half-finished edit never reaches your server.

## Which Minecraft versions to tick

Each version you tick is a separate build of the pack.

- On an ordinary server, everybody is on the server's version, so tick that.
- With [ViaVersion](https://viaversion.com), players can join on other
  versions. Tick each one you want to support, and see
  [Players on many versions](#players-on-many-versions) below.
- Bedrock players joining through Geyser get a Bedrock version of the pack
  automatically, if the pack has anything Bedrock can show. See
  [Bedrock and Geyser](/bedrock).

The version breakdown on the Distribution page shows which versions your
players are really on, so you can tick any you missed and publish again.

<Callout type="note" title="Brand-new Minecraft versions">
  A version we haven't added yet shows as unrecognised. Those players start
  getting the pack once we add it; you don't need to do anything.
</Callout>

## Seeing who got it

The Distribution page shows downloads per day for the last 30 days, which
versions your players are on, recent joins, and each bound server with the
last time it served somebody.

**Watch the Declined count.** Players can turn off server resource packs in
their server list settings (*Server Resource Packs*) and never see a prompt,
and most don't know the setting is there. A high decline count is usually
that.

Player names and UUIDs come from your server. On an offline-mode server they
can be faked, so unique-player counts there are an estimate.

## Pausing and stopping

The **Manage** card at the bottom of the Distribution page has two buttons:

- **Pause** stops your servers handing out the pack, and keeps everything
  else. **Resume** starts again with no rebuild.
- **Stop distributing** deletes the release, disconnects every server and
  clears the numbers. The pack itself is kept, and you can start again from
  **Serve a pack**. Only the pack's owner can do this.

To rename a bound server, press the pencil beside its name. To disconnect just
one, press the bin beside it.

## On your server

When you publish, RP Engine puts the pack in its content folder (the folder of
packs it builds and sends to players) as
`plugins/RPEngine/content/studio-<pack>/`. It's merged with anything else you
have there, so players still download one pack. `/rp shaders`, `/rp dialogs`
and the rest list what's in it.

Each new release replaces that folder within a minute. Make changes in Studio,
not in the folder: files you edit there by hand are copied to
`plugins/RPEngine/studio-backups/` and then replaced.

| Command | What happens |
|---|---|
| `/rp distribute off` | Disconnects the server and removes the folder |
| `/rp distribute off --keep` | Disconnects the server and keeps the folder as your own content, no longer updated |

This needs RP Engine 0.2.49 or newer. Older versions send the published pack
to each player separately instead of writing the folder.

### Players on many versions

The folder holds the build for your server's own version (or the nearest one
below it that you published), and that's what every Java player gets. If your
players join on many versions through ViaVersion, set
`distribution.mode: cdn` in RP Engine's [config](/rp-engine/config#studio):
each player is then sent the build for their own version. A player on a version you
didn't tick is then sent nothing, and shows up in the version breakdown.

**Shader objects or Dialogs not showing?** They need RP Engine 0.2.38 or newer
and a release published on or after 29 October 2026. Press **New release** if
yours is older.

## Who can publish

On your own packs, you. A [collaborator](/collaborators) can see the
Distribution numbers if you give them that section, but publishing needs the
**Publish to servers** permission, which you grant separately. On a
[team](/teams), owners and admins can publish; members can't.

## Limits

Every plan can distribute. Starter serves up to 50 different players a month,
counted across all the packs you own. Creator, Studio and Team are unlimited.
Your current usage is on the Distribution page.
