# Distribution

Distribution sends your pack to everyone who joins your Minecraft server, built
for the version *they* are running, and shows you who actually took it. You
bind your server to a pack once, then publish whenever the pack is ready.

This is different from [testing](/testing/plugin), which pushes a pack to one
player while you build it. Distribution is the pack your server runs.

You need [RP Engine](/testing/plugin) on the server.

## Setting it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Serve a pack">
    Open **Distribution** in the sidebar, press **Serve a pack**, and choose
    which pack the server should hand its players.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bind your server">
    You get an eight-digit code, good for 30 minutes. On your server, as an
    operator (or anyone with `rpengine.distribute`), run:

    ```
    /rp distribute 12345678
    ```

    The server remembers this. It picks up everything you publish afterwards
    on its own, with no restart. `/rp distribute off` unbinds it. A pack can
    be bound to up to 10 servers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Press **Publish**, tick the Minecraft versions you want to support, and
    confirm. Every ticked version is built up front, so a joining player never
    waits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Players get the pack the next time they join.

## Versions

You choose which versions to support; nothing is guessed. Each version is its
own download, and the server picks one for each player as they join:

- On an ordinary server, everyone is on the server's version.
- With [ViaVersion](https://viaversion.com) installed, players can be on many
  versions at once, and each gets the matching build.
- Bedrock players joining through Geyser get the Bedrock build, if your pack
  has anything Bedrock can use. See [Exporting › Bedrock](/exporting#bedrock).

A player on a version you haven't ticked is **served nothing and recorded**, so
they show up in the version breakdown and you can tick that version and
publish again.

<Callout type="note" title="Brand-new Minecraft versions">
  A version we haven't added yet is reported as unrecognised rather than
  guessed at, because a pack built for the wrong version loads and then draws
  nothing. Those players start working once we add it; you don't need to do
  anything.
</Callout>

## Publishing again

There is **one live release**. Publishing again builds your ticked versions
fresh and replaces it, and the previous build is removed. There's no rollback.

What your players get is the last thing you published, not what's in the
editor right now, so saving a half-finished edit never reaches a live server.

Shader objects, dialogs and screens in the pack work for every player who was
served it: `/rp shaders` lists them and their rules apply. That needs RP Engine
0.2.38 or newer and a release published after 29 October 2026, so publish
again if yours is older.

## What you can see

The Distribution page shows serves per day over the last 30 days, which
versions your players are on, recent joins, and each bound server with the last
time it served somebody.

**Watch the Declined count.** A client can refuse server packs without the
player ever seeing a prompt (the *Server Resource Packs* setting in the
server list), and most people don't know it's there. A high decline rate is
usually that.

Player names and UUIDs come from your server. On an offline-mode server UUIDs
can be faked, so unique-player counts there are an estimate.

## Who can publish

On your own packs, you. A [collaborator](/collaborators) can see the
Distribution numbers with that section, but publishing needs the **Publish to
servers** capability, which is never included in the Editor preset. On a
[team](/teams), owners and admins can publish; members can't.

## Limits

Every plan can distribute. Starter serves up to 50 unique players a month,
counted across the packs you own. Creator, Studio and Team are unlimited. Your
current usage is on the Distribution page.
