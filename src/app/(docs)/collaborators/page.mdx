# Collaborators

A pack starts as yours alone. **Collaborators** are other accounts you let into
it: a builder doing your models, a friend drawing block textures, a client
watching without touching anything.

Collaborators belong to **one pack**. Adding somebody to your castle pack gives
them nothing in your other packs. If you want several people sharing every
pack and one pool of credits, use [Teams](/teams) instead.

A pack can have **five collaborators** besides you. A pending invitation holds
one of those places until it's accepted, declined, withdrawn or expires.

## Adding somebody

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pack's Collaborators page">
    The people icon in the pack header, beside **Edit** and **Export**. Only
    the pack's owner sees it. Then press **Add collaborator**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find them by Minecraft username or email">
    **Minecraft username** finds an account that has linked that name with
    `/permlink` on <ServerAddress /> (see
    [Our test server](/testing/test-server#linking-your-account)).

    **Email** works for anyone, including people without a resourcepack.ai
    account, who can create one from the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what they can reach">
    **Editor** is the full run of the pack: edit, generate, sync and export.
    **Viewer** can open everything and change nothing. **Custom** lets you
    pick the sections and capabilities below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    They get an email, plus a Discord DM and a line of in-game chat if they've
    turned those on. Invitations last **14 days** and can be withdrawn from
    the same page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Inviting by email always says "invitation sent", whether or not the address
has an account, so the box can't be used to find out who has signed up.
Collaborators never see each other's email addresses; they appear as a
Minecraft name and head.

## What a collaborator can do

Two independent choices, so "can generate but can't delete my work" is
possible, and so is the reverse.

**Which sections**, matching the tabs on the pack page: Models, Vehicles,
Blocks, Items, Armor, GUIs, HUD, Dialogs, Icons, Sounds, Emotes, Shaders, and
Distribution. A section they aren't given simply isn't there for them.

**What they can do in those sections:**

| Capability | Lets them |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Create, edit, rename and delete assets by hand |
| **Generate with AI** | Run AI generation and edits, which spend credits (see below) |
| **Sync & test in game** | Push the pack to Minecraft and run give commands |
| **Export** | Download the pack as a `.zip` or export single models |
| **Publish to servers** | Publish a release to the servers bound to this pack with [Distribution](/distribution) |

**Publish to servers** is never part of Editor. Give it by name with Custom
when you want someone to be able to update a live server.

Some things stay yours whatever you grant: renaming, deleting or duplicating
the pack, changing its icon, moving assets to or from other packs, choosing
whose credits it spends, and managing collaborators.

## Whose credits get spent

Set in **Edit pack**, under **AI Credits for this pack**:

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Use my credits** (default) | Whatever a collaborator generates comes out of your balance |
| **Everyone uses their own** | Collaborators spend their own credits. Yours stay untouched |

Either way your billing history records who ran each action. On a team pack
this setting doesn't apply to team members, who always spend the team's pool.

## Testing as a collaborator

Sync works the same for a collaborator and sends the pack to **whoever pressed
it**. Link your own Minecraft account with `/permlink` and Sync lands on you,
not on the pack's owner. To test on your own server instead, each collaborator
pairs with their own code (see [The plugin](/testing/plugin)).

## Hearing about what they do

**Notifications** on the Collaborators page chooses what you're told about and
where, using the same three channels as
[generation notifications](/generating#notifications): email, Discord DM and
in-game chat. Set it for the whole pack, or override it per person from the
gear beside their name.

| Event | On by default |
| --- | --- |
| Adds something | |
| Edits something | |
| Deletes something | Yes |
| Runs AI generation | Yes |
| Syncs to Minecraft | |
| Exports the pack | |
| Accepts collaboration invite | Yes |

Every event is sent as it happens, so turning on **Edits something** for a busy
collaborator means a message per save.

## Changing or removing access

The gear beside a collaborator's name opens their grant, so you can change what
they reach. **Remove from pack** ends their access and their sync code for the
pack straight away. Anything they made stays in the pack.
