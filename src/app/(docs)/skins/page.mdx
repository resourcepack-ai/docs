# Skins

A skin is what your player looks like in Minecraft. In Studio you can
generate one from a description, start from an existing skin, or paint one by
hand, then wear it in game.

Skins live on your account rather than in a pack, under **Skins** in the
sidebar, because a skin is yours wherever you play.

![A chef skin made in Studio, from the front, walking and from the back](/images/skins.webp)

## Making one

Press **New Skin** and choose how to start:

- **Generate**: describe it ("a knight in dented steel armour with a red
  cloak") and Studio paints it, starting from the default skin so the face and
  shape are right. It costs AI Credits (from 45) and takes a few minutes; the
  skin shows as generating until it lands.
- **Blank**: open the editor on an **Empty body**, a **Default skin** (one of
  Minecraft's own characters) or a **Player skin** (type a Minecraft username
  to start from that player's skin). Free.
- **Upload**: drop a skin PNG you already have.

Every skin has an **arm width**, **Classic** (4px arms) or **Slim** (3px), which
you can change later.

**Edit with AI** on a skin's page describes a change ("give him a green
hood") and makes an edited copy, so the original stays as it was. It costs
credits like generating.

## The editor

You paint the skin flat, beside a 3D player you can turn to check it.
The **outer layer** sits just off the body, which is how hair, hoods, sleeves
and jackets stand proud of it; switch to it to paint there. The tools are the
pixel editor's: pencil, fill, a colour picker, rectangles and ellipses, and
**Fill part** to colour a whole body part at once.

## Using it

There are two ways to wear a skin, and they do different things:

| | Sync | Apply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where you wear it | On our test server | Everywhere you play, until you change it |
| Who sees it | Everybody on the test server | Everybody, on every server |
| Changes your Minecraft account | No | Yes, once you upload it |

### Trying it on with Sync

**Sync** on a skin's page puts it on you straight away, without leaving the
game. It needs your Minecraft account linked to Studio (see the
[Quickstart](/quickstart#see-it-in-minecraft)) and you online on
[our test server](/testing/test-server) when you press it. Everybody there sees
the new skin.

If a sync is refused on a busy day, wait a minute and try again: the service
that makes skins visible to other players has a rate limit.

### Wearing it everywhere

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Apply">
    Press **Apply** on the skin's page (or in the editor). Studio downloads the
    64×64 PNG Minecraft uses and opens the skin page on minecraft.net in a new
    tab. **Export** downloads the PNG without opening anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it to your account">
    Sign in there, choose the same arm width as in Studio (**Classic** or
    **Slim**), and upload the PNG.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rejoin">
    Your skin changes the next time you join a world or server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Sharing it

**Publish to the Gallery** on a skin's page lets anybody copy it into their
own account.
