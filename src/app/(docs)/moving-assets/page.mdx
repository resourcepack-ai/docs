# Moving assets between packs

You can copy or move anything you've made into another pack, one asset at a
time or many at once, without generating it again. **Copy** leaves the
original where it is. **Move** takes it out of this pack.

## Copying or moving

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to send">
    For **one asset**, press **Move to pack** on its page, or right-click it
    in the pack grid. For **several**, tick them in the grid (the checkbox
    appears top-left when you hover a card) and press **Move to pack** in the
    bar that comes up. **Select all** ticks everything on the tab you're on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the pack">
    Choose an existing pack, or type a name to make a **new** one on the
    spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Move or Copy">
    Then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes on the way

Most assets arrive exactly as they were. A few details are given out once per
pack, so they're reassigned in the new one:

| What | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A model's **carrier** (the vanilla item or block the game draws as your model) | Reassigned in the new pack. Same kind where one is free, otherwise the item carrier |
| A GUI overlay's **characters** | New ones, so the title text that opens the GUI changes. Copy it again from the GUI's page |
| An icon's **character** | Kept, unless another icon in the target pack already uses it |
| The textures a model **uses** | Copied along with it, so it doesn't arrive as a black-and-magenta checkerboard |

<Callout type="note" title="Reskins can't sit side by side">
  A block or item reskin is named after the vanilla thing it replaces, so a
  pack can only hold one of each. If the target already has that block
  reskinned, yours is left behind and you're told so. Tick **Overwrite** in the
  sheet if you meant to replace it.
</Callout>
