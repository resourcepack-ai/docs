# Loading Screens

A Loading Screen restyles the screen Minecraft shows while resource packs load:
normally red, with the Mojang Studios logo and a white bar. Yours can have its
own colors, your server's name and logo, an effect like falling snow or rising
embers, and a progress bar to match.

![Six Loading Screens made in Studio: a pastel marshmallow, a Halloween pumpkin, a beach sunset, a winter snowflake, an arcade joystick and a racing flag](/images/loading-screens.webp)

## Making one

Press **Add asset** and pick the **Loading Screen** tab. Either:

- **Describe it** ("a spooky Halloween server called Grimfall"). Studio designs
  the colors, effect, words and bar, which costs 5 AI Credits and takes a few
  seconds; or
- **Pick a look**, which is free: Winter Night, Nether, Starfield, Firefly
  Forest, Thunderstorm, Royal Gold, Ocean, Clean, or Recolored (Minecraft's
  own screen and logo in a color of your choosing). The name you give it
  becomes the title on the screen.

Either way it opens in the editor.

## The editor

The screen plays live, as the game draws it: the effects move and the bar
fills. A Loading Screen is made of **layers**, listed on the left; click one
(or click it on the screen) to change it on the right. Press **+** (or N) to
add a layer, drag layers in the list to reorder them, and press Delete to
remove one.

- **Words**: a title, or a smaller line such as your server's address, as
  many as six with the pictures. Each has its own font (Chunky, the default;
  Classic; or Tiny, which keeps lower case), size, color or two-color
  gradient, outline, bold and letter spacing. They stack top to bottom in the
  order listed.
- **Pictures**: your logo as pixel art, up to two. Best up to 128 by 64 pixels
  and 12 colors; bigger ones are shrunk to fit.
- **Effects**: snow, rain, stars, embers, sparkles, fireflies, bubbles,
  petals, hearts or confetti, in any color and amount, up to three at once.
- **Minecraft's logo**: keep the Mojang Studios logo and restyle the screen
  round it.
- **Background**: one color, a gradient (top to bottom, out from the middle,
  or corner to corner), or a pattern of two colors (stripes, a checkerboard,
  or a sunburst that slowly turns), with an optional glow behind the logo.
- **Bar**: the frame and fill colors, solid, a gradient along the bar,
  moving stripes or segments, and an optional color for its empty part.
- **Logo**: the size of the whole logo, how high it sits, the pictures beside
  the words instead of above them, and a shadow under everything, from a drop
  shadow to a deep 3D edge.
- **Looks**: switch to another ready-made style. Your words and pictures stay.
- **Edit with AI**: say what to change ("make it purple", "add fireflies") and
  the layers change. Nothing is saved until you press Save, so you can undo it.

A pack shows one Loading Screen. If it has several, the card marked **Shown**
is the one in the pack; press **Use this** on another to switch.

## When players see it

It works on **Minecraft 26.1, 26.2 and 26.3**. Older versions see Minecraft's
own screen. On 26.1 the effect stands still, because that version gives the
loading screen no clock; it moves on 26.2 and 26.3.

A player sees it whenever the game reloads resources while your pack is
already loaded:

- pressing F3+T;
- turning packs on or off in the Resource Packs menu;
- a server sending a second pack, or leaving a server whose pack is then
  taken off.

When a player joins your server, the load starts on Minecraft's red screen,
because the game hasn't read your pack yet, and switches to yours part-way
through. The very first screen when the game starts is always Minecraft's
own.

## How it works

Minecraft reads the Mojang logo from its own files only, so a resource pack
can't simply replace it. Instead your pack overrides the two shaders the
loading screen is drawn with, recognizes the background, the logo and the bar
by where they are, and paints your design over them. Everything else drawn
with those shaders is left exactly as it was. The shaders only load on the
versions above, so a newer or older Minecraft falls back to the normal screen
rather than breaking your pack.

## Sharing it

**Publish** in the editor's header puts it in the
[Gallery](https://resourcepack.ai/gallery), where it plays live on its page.
Anybody can add it to a pack of their own, where it becomes that pack's
Loading Screen, and edit it like any other; pictures in its logo go with it.
