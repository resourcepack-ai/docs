# Teams

A **team** is a shared workspace. Every pack it owns belongs to everybody on it,
everybody spends from one pool of AI Credits, and it's all on one invoice.

It's different from [Collaborators](/collaborators). A collaborator is somebody
you let into **one pack**, with a chosen slice of it. A team is for people who
work on everything together, where inviting each of them to every pack one at a
time would be a chore.

<Callout type="note">
  The two work together. Everyone **on** the team gets every team pack
  automatically. Someone **outside** it can still be invited to a single team
  pack as a collaborator, and doesn't take a seat.
</Callout>

## What it costs

**$30 per seat, per month**, with a minimum of two seats and a maximum of 50.

| | Per seat |
| --- | --- |
| AI Credits added to the pool each month | 1,000 |
| Packs | 20 (so a two-seat team has 40) |
| Distribution players | Unlimited |

The credits are one shared pool, spent by whoever needs them. Top-ups are
**40% off** on a team, the best rate there is; only the owner can buy them.

<Callout type="note">
  **A seat doesn't replace your own plan.** They bill separately and you can
  hold both. Your own packs run on your plan, the team's packs on the team's.
</Callout>

## Switching between your packs and the team's

Click your name and credit balance at the bottom left of the sidebar. The list
shows **Personal** and every team you're on.

Whatever you pick decides what the rest of the app shows: the pack list, the
**New pack** button, the credit balance and the limits all follow it.

**A pack belongs to the context it was made in.** Make one while the team is
selected and it's the team's, visible to everybody on it. Make one in Personal
and it's yours. Packs can't be moved between the two.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can, plus billing: seats, top-ups, automatic top-ups, cancelling. One per team |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove people, change roles, set limits, publish and delete packs, manage keys and webhooks, see Activity and Billing |
| **Member** | Edit, generate, sync and export in every team pack, spending from the pool |
| **Viewer** | Open every team pack and change nothing: no editing, generating, syncing or exporting. For clients and contractors |

Membership is the access: there's nothing to set up per person. Admins keep
the two actions that are hard to undo, **publishing** a pack to players with
[Distribution](/distribution) and **deleting** a pack.

## Seats

You buy seats, then fill them. Removing a person frees their seat at once, and
you can give it to someone else at no extra cost.

<Callout type="note">
  **An empty seat is still a paid seat.** Removing a person doesn't lower your
  bill. Reducing the seat count is a separate step on the team's Billing page.
</Callout>

- **Adding a seat** takes effect straight away. Your card is charged the
  prorated rest of the month (the confirmation shows the exact amount), and
  the pool gets the same share of that seat's 1,000 credits. The full 1,000
  arrives at renewal.
- **Removing a seat** offers **Now** or **At renewal**. Neither refunds
  anything, so pick **At renewal** unless you want the lower number today.

## Inviting somebody

On the team's **Members** page, invite them by email. Invitations last **seven
days** and can be withdrawn at any time. An outstanding invitation holds a seat,
so you can't send more invitations than you have free seats.

Inviting by email always says "invitation sent", whether or not the address has
an account, so nobody can use the box to find out who's signed up.

## Limiting someone to certain packs

An admin can restrict a member or viewer to chosen packs from the members list.
Nobody is restricted by default.

It's an allowlist: a pack made **after** you set it isn't included, so a
contractor doesn't quietly gain next month's project. Admins and the owner
can't be restricted.

## Spend limits

An admin can cap how many credits one person takes from the pool in a billing
period, from the members list. Blank means no limit, which is the default.
Everyone can see their own limit and how much of it they've used. The owner
can't be limited.

## API keys and your own OpenAI key

Both follow the context you're in.

- **API and MCP keys** made on the Developers page while the team is selected
  belong to the team: they spend the pool and reach the team's packs. Only
  admins can create and revoke them. If someone leaves, every team key they
  held stops working. A key made in Personal never reaches a team's packs.
  See the [API reference](/api-reference).
- **Your own OpenAI key**: an admin can store one for the team, and 3D model
  generation and edits in team packs then run on it instead of the pool. A
  team pack always uses the team's key, never a member's personal one.

## Activity

The team's **Activity** page, for admins and the owner, has two tabs:

- **Audit log**: invitations, joins and removals, role changes, ownership
  transfers, limits and pack restrictions, seat changes and top-ups, keys,
  packs created, published or deleted, and team renames.
- **Spend**: credits each person has used this billing period, and how many
  actions that was.

**Export CSV** downloads either, for all time or a date range you choose, and
the audit log can be filtered by kind of action.

## Automatic top-ups

When the pool runs out, everybody stops at once. The owner can set a floor and
an amount (500 to 20,000 credits): when the balance drops below the floor,
that many credits are bought on the team's card. At most three automatic
purchases happen per billing period, each one emails the owner and appears in
the audit log, and a team whose plan has lapsed is never charged.

## Webhooks

Admins can send everything the audit log records to a URL, as JSON, to get it
into Discord, Slack or your own tools. Add up to five on the team's
**Settings** page. Each has its own signing secret, shown once when you add it,
and every event goes to all of them.

### The request

Each event is a `POST`:

```
Content-Type: application/json
User-Agent: resourcepack.ai-webhook/1
X-RPAI-Signature: sha256=<hex>
```

```json
{
  "type": "pack.published",
  "team": "a1b2c3d4",
  "actor": "user_9f8e7d",
  "target": "ruined_kingdom",
  "detail": { "version": 3 },
  "id": "b7c1e0f2-…",
  "at": "2026-08-23T14:02:11.904Z"
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `type` | The action, using the audit log's names (`member.joined`, `member.role_changed`, `seat.changed`, `pack.published` and so on) |
| `actor` | The account id that did it, or `null` |
| `target` | What it happened to, such as a pack or a person |
| `detail` | Extra fields for that type, or `null` |
| `id` | Unique per event, and the same across your webhooks. Use it to spot duplicates |
| `at` | When it was sent, ISO 8601 |

### Verifying the signature

`X-RPAI-Signature` is `sha256=` plus the hex **HMAC-SHA256** of the **raw
request body**, keyed with your signing secret. Compute it over the bytes you
received, not a re-serialized copy of the parsed JSON.

```js
import { createHmac, timingSafeEqual } from "node:crypto";

function verify(rawBody, header, secret) {
  const expected = "sha256=" + createHmac("sha256", secret).update(rawBody).digest("hex");
  const a = Buffer.from(header ?? "");
  const b = Buffer.from(expected);
  return a.length === b.length && timingSafeEqual(a, b);
}
```

### Delivery

HTTPS only, never to loopback or private-network addresses, and redirects
aren't followed. Each attempt times out after five seconds and **is not
retried**: if your endpoint is down, that event is lost. The CSV export is the
complete record. The **Settings** card shows the last delivery's status.

## Leaving

Anyone except the owner can leave from the team's page, and an admin can remove
anyone except the owner. To leave as the owner, first press **Make owner** on
someone in the members list: billing moves to them and you become an admin.

**Packs stay with the team.** Nobody takes a pack with them when they leave.

If the team's plan lapses, nothing is deleted. Packs and access stay, and so do
the credits in the pool; only new paid work stops, as it would for a personal
account.
