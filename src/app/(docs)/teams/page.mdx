# Teams

A **team** is a free shared workspace for a group that makes packs together,
such as a server's build team or a small studio. Every pack the team owns
belongs to everybody on it, and generating in those packs uses the team's AI
Credits (the app's currency for generating), which anyone on the team can top
up.

If you only want to let someone into **one pack**, you don't need a team:
invite them as a [collaborator](/collaborators) instead.

<Callout type="note">
  The two work together. Everyone **on** the team gets every team pack
  automatically. Someone **outside** it can still be invited to a single team
  pack as a collaborator.
</Callout>

## What it costs

**Nothing.** Creating a team is free, and there are no seats or monthly price.
A team can hold up to 50 people, counting pending invitations.

| | Per team |
| --- | --- |
| Packs | 50 |
| Distribution players | Unlimited |

A team starts with no AI Credits. See [Team credits](#team-credits) for how it
gets them.

<Callout type="note">
  **A team doesn't replace your own plan.** You can be on a team and have your
  own plan. Your own packs use your credits, and the team's packs use the
  team's.
</Callout>

## Starting a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create a Team">
    Click your name and credit balance at the bottom left of the sidebar,
    then **Create a Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Press **Create Team**. The team is selected and its Members page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite people">
    Press **Invite** on the Members page (see [below](#inviting-somebody)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching between your packs and the team's

Click your name and credit balance at the bottom left of the sidebar. The list
shows **Personal** and every team you're on.

Whatever you pick decides what the rest of the app shows: the pack list, the
**New pack** button, the credit balance and the limits all follow it.

**A pack belongs to the context it was made in.** Make one while the team is
selected and it's the team's, visible to everybody on it. Make one in Personal
and it's yours. Packs can't be moved between the two.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can, plus handing the team over and deleting it. One per team |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove people, change roles, set limits, publish and delete packs, manage keys and webhooks, see Activity |
| **Member** | Edit, generate, sync and export in every team pack, and top up the team's credits |
| **Viewer** | Open every team pack and change nothing: no editing, generating, syncing or exporting. For clients and contractors |

Being on the team is the access: there's nothing to set up per person. Admins
keep the two actions that are hard to undo: **publishing** a pack to a live
server's players with [Distribution](/distribution), and **deleting** a pack.

## Team credits

Every generation in a team pack uses the **team's credits**, whoever presses
Generate. The balance is on the team's **Credits** page, which everyone except
viewers can open.

**Anyone on the team can top it up** from that page. You pay with your own
card, at your own plan's rate: if your plan gives you 30% off top-ups, you get
30% off topping up the team too. The audit log records who added what.

<Callout type="note">
  Credits you add to a team stay with the team. If you leave, they don't come
  with you.
</Callout>

## Using your own credits

You can choose to pay for your own generations in a team's packs instead of
using the team's credits. Open the team's **Credits** page and press **Use my
own credits**.

- It applies to every generation you make in that team's packs until you
  switch back on the same page.
- What you make still belongs to the team.
- It only changes who pays for **your** generations. Everyone else keeps using
  the team's credits.
- Spend limits don't apply to you while it's on, because you're not spending
  the team's credits.
- The team's admins see when you switch, in the audit log.

If the team runs out while you're generating, the out-of-credits window offers
both: top up the team, or switch to your own credits there and then.

## Inviting somebody

On the team's **Members** page, invite them by email. Invitations last **seven
days** and can be withdrawn at any time. A team can hold 50 people, and an
outstanding invitation counts towards that.

## Limiting someone to certain packs

An admin can restrict a member or viewer to chosen packs from the members list.
Nobody is restricted by default.

It's an allowlist: a pack made **after** you set it isn't included, so a
contractor doesn't quietly gain next month's project. Admins and the owner
can't be restricted.

## Spend limits

An admin can cap how many of the team's credits one person uses in a calendar
month, from the members list. Blank means no limit, which is the default.
Everyone can see their own limit and how much of it they've used. The owner
can't be limited, and the limit doesn't apply to anyone
[using their own credits](#using-your-own-credits).

## API keys and your own OpenAI key

Skip this unless you use the API or bring your own key. Both follow whichever
context (Personal or the team) is selected.

- **API and MCP keys** made on the Developers page while the team is selected
  belong to the team: they spend the team's credits and reach the team's packs. Only
  admins can create and revoke them. If someone leaves, every team key they
  held stops working. A key made in Personal never reaches a team's packs.
  See the [API reference](/api-reference).
- **Your own OpenAI key**: an admin can store one for the team, and 3D model
  generation and edits in team packs then run on it instead of the team's
  credits. A
  team pack always uses the team's key, never a member's personal one.

## Activity

The team's **Activity** page, for admins and the owner, has two tabs:

- **Audit log**: invitations, joins and removals, role changes, ownership
  transfers, limits and pack restrictions, top-ups, switches to and from
  people's own credits, keys, packs created, published or deleted, and team
  renames.
- **Spend**: the team's credits each person has used this month, how many
  actions that was, and what they spent of their own credits in team packs.

**Export CSV** downloads either, for all time or a date range you choose, and
the audit log can be filtered by kind of action.

## Webhooks

This is for developers. Admins can send everything the audit log records to a URL, as JSON, to get it
into Discord, Slack or your own tools. Add up to five on the team's
**Settings** page. Each has its own signing secret, shown once when you add it,
and every event goes to all of them.

### The request

Each event is a `POST`:

```
Content-Type: application/json
User-Agent: resourcepack.ai-webhook/1
X-RPAI-Signature: sha256=<hex>
```

```json
{
  "type": "pack.published",
  "team": "a1b2c3d4",
  "actor": "user_9f8e7d",
  "target": "ruined_kingdom",
  "detail": { "version": 3 },
  "id": "b7c1e0f2-…",
  "at": "2026-08-23T14:02:11.904Z"
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `type` | The action, using the audit log's names (`member.joined`, `member.role_changed`, `credits.topped_up`, `pack.published` and so on) |
| `actor` | The account id that did it, or `null` |
| `target` | What it happened to, such as a pack or a person |
| `detail` | Extra fields for that type, or `null` |
| `id` | Unique per event, and the same across your webhooks. Use it to spot duplicates |
| `at` | When it was sent, ISO 8601 |

### Verifying the signature

`X-RPAI-Signature` is `sha256=` plus the hex **HMAC-SHA256** of the **raw
request body**, keyed with your signing secret. Compute it over the bytes you
received, not a re-serialized copy of the parsed JSON.

```js
import { createHmac, timingSafeEqual } from "node:crypto";

function verify(rawBody, header, secret) {
  const expected = "sha256=" + createHmac("sha256", secret).update(rawBody).digest("hex");
  const a = Buffer.from(header ?? "");
  const b = Buffer.from(expected);
  return a.length === b.length && timingSafeEqual(a, b);
}
```

### Delivery

HTTPS only, never to loopback or private-network addresses, and redirects
aren't followed. Each attempt times out after five seconds and **is not
retried**: if your endpoint is down, that event is lost. The CSV export is the
complete record. The **Settings** card shows the last delivery's status.

## Leaving

Anyone except the owner can leave from the team's page, and an admin can remove
anyone except the owner. To leave as the owner, first press **Make owner** on
someone in the members list: they become the owner and you become an admin.

**Packs and credits stay with the team.** Nobody takes a pack or the team's
credits with them when they leave.
