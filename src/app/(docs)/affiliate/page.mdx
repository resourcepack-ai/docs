# Affiliate programme

Every account has an affiliate link. People who create an account after
following it become your referrals, and **40% of what they spend in their
first year comes back to you as AI Credits**.

There's nothing to apply for. Open **Affiliate** in the sidebar and your link
is there.

## Your link

Your link is the front page with your username on it:

```
https://resourcepack.ai/?ref=yourname
```

**Copy link** on the Affiliate page copies it. If you haven't set a username,
one is made the first time you open the page; you can change it on
**Account**, and the link changes with it.

<Callout type="note">
  Someone counts as your referral if they **create an account within 60 days**
  of following your link. They don't have to sign up straight away or on the
  page they landed on. If they follow someone else's link before signing up,
  the most recent link wins.
</Callout>

Who referred an account is settled when the account is created and never
changes afterwards. An existing account can't be claimed later.

## Earning from the gallery

Anything you publish to the [Gallery](https://resourcepack.ai/gallery) carries
your code. Anyone who signs up from one of your gallery pages is your
referral, with no link to share.

Items published **anonymously** earn nothing, because there's no creator to
credit. The Affiliate page shows how many of your published items are earning
and how many are anonymous.

## What you earn

40% of what a referred account pays, for 12 months from the day they signed
up:

| They buy | You earn |
| --- | --- |
| A subscription | 40% of every month they pay for, not only the first |
| A credit top-up | 40% of it |
| Supporter | 40% of it |
| Nothing | Nothing |

It's paid in AI Credits at the standard credit price ($0.05 a credit), rounded
down to whole credits. They never expire and work like any other credits.

The 12 months start when the account is created, not at their first purchase:
someone who signs up today and subscribes in six months earns you six months of
commission.

## When the credits land

Each commission is **Pending** for 30 days, with its release date shown, and
then becomes **Paid** and joins your balance. The wait covers the window in
which a card payment can be refunded or charged back.

If a payment is refunded while its commission is pending, the commission shows
as **Refunded** and pays nothing. A commission that has already been paid stays
yours. The **Earnings** list on the Affiliate page has one line per payment.

## Rules

- **Referring yourself doesn't work.** A new account on the same device as the
  affiliate has its referral voided.
- **You don't see who your referrals are.** The page shows how many there are
  and how many have paid, never names, usernames or emails.
- **Credits only.** There's no cash payout.

Sharing your link means agreeing to the
[affiliate terms](https://resourcepack.ai/terms#affiliate): no self-referral,
no spam, and nothing untrue about us.
