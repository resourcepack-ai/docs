# Affiliate programme

Anyone with an account has an affiliate link. People who create an account
after following it are yours, and **40% of everything they go on to spend
comes back to you as AI Credits** for their first year.

There's nothing to apply for and nothing to enrol in. Open **Affiliate** in
the sidebar and your link is already there.

## Your link

Your link is your username on the front page:

```
https://resourcepack.ai/?ref=yourname
```

The **Copy link** button on the Affiliate page gives you exactly that. If you
haven't set a username yet, one is made for you the first time you open the
page; you can change it on **Account**, and the link changes with it.

<Callout type="note">
  Somebody counts as your referral if they **create an account within 60 days**
  of following your link. They don't have to sign up in the same visit, or on
  the same page — the link can point anywhere on the site, and browsing around
  first doesn't lose the attribution. If they follow somebody else's link
  before signing up, the most recent one wins.
</Callout>

Attribution is settled once, when the account is created, and never changes
after that. An account belongs to one affiliate for good, which also means a
referral can't be claimed after the fact.

## The gallery earns for you too

The second way to be credited needs no link at all. Publish an asset to the
[Gallery](https://resourcepack.ai/gallery) and its page carries your code:
anyone who signs up from something you published is your referral, exactly as
if they'd followed a link you sent them.

  you can see it rather than wonder.
</Callout>

## What you earn

**40% of what a referred account pays us, for 12 months from the day they
signed up.** Everything they buy counts:

| What they buy | Earns you |
| --- | --- |
| **A subscription** | 40% of every month they pay for, not just the first |
| **A credit top-up** | 40% of the purchase |
| **Supporter** | 40% of it |
| **Nothing** | Nothing — a free account earns you nothing until it buys something |

It's paid in **AI Credits**, converted at the standard credit price, and those
credits never expire. They're ordinary credits: spend them on anything you
generate.

<Callout type="note">
  The 12 months run from the day the account was created, not from their first
  purchase. Somebody who signs up today and subscribes in six months still
  earns for you for the six after that.
</Callout>

## When the credits land

Every commission is **held for 30 days** before it becomes spendable, and shows
as **Pending** with its release date until then. It's not a review period — a
card payment can still be refunded or charged back weeks after it goes through,
and credits already spent on a generation can't be taken back.

After that it flips to **Paid** and the credits are in your balance, where they
behave like any others. The Earnings list on the Affiliate page is one line per
payment, with the state of each.

If a payment is refunded, the commission for it is voided while it's still
pending, and shows as **Refunded** with no credits. One that has already landed
stays yours.

## What doesn't count

<Callout type="danger">
  **Referring yourself doesn't work.** A new account made on the same device as
  the affiliate it credits has its referral voided, and it stays on record. The
  arithmetic doesn't favour it either: a free account earns nothing at all
  until it pays for something, so there's nothing to farm.
</Callout>

Also worth knowing:

- **You never see who your referrals are.** The page shows how many there are
  and how many have paid, and no names, usernames or email addresses — who
  signed up is theirs to tell you, not ours.
- **Credits, not cash.** The programme pays in AI Credits and only in AI
  Credits; there's no payout to a bank account.

Sharing your link means agreeing to the
