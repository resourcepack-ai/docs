# Skateboards

An **addon** for [RP Engine](/rp-engine): a plugin that installs into the
engine and adds one thing, rather than a pack you author yourself. Drop it in
beside the engine and it brings its own content with it.

This one is a skateboard you can ride: push it along, tuck for speed, ollie,
grind, lie down on it and ride a wall. It also comes with a skatepark to ride it
in: ramps, rails, boxes, platforms and the park furniture, all placeable and
all skateable.

## Installing

Drop `RPE-skateboards.jar` in `plugins/` beside `RPEngine.jar` and start the
server. Its content (the board's model, textures, item, vehicle definition
and rider animations, and every skatepark piece) installs itself into
`plugins/RPEngine/content/skateboards/` on first boot, and the engine reloads
once to pick it up. That folder is yours: edit anything in it, and a new
version of the plugin overwrites only its own files.

## Riding

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **W** | Push. A kick every 0.7 s, up to a speed the legs cannot beat |
| **S** | Push it backwards with the front foot, once you have stopped |
| **A/D** | Steer. In the air, flick the board round under you |
| **Sprint** | Tuck. The speed cap comes off and the board creeps toward its top speed |
| **Space** | Ollie. Held against a wall, ride the wall |
| **Shift** (hold) | The coffin: lie flat on your back along the board |
| **Shift** (tap) | Step off |

Two more worth knowing:

- **Stance.** `/skateboard stance left|right` names the foot you lead with. It
  is remembered per player and for every board you ever step on, and a
  right-footed rider gets the mirrored copy of every animation.
- **Bailing.** Land a long way round from the way you are travelling and you
  come off. Straight is fine, and so is straight backwards: that is a fakie,
  not a fall. You take half a heart.

## The skatepark

`/skatepark` lists the pieces, `/skatepark <piece>` hands one over, and
`/skatepark all` gives you the lot. Right-click the ground to place one, punch
it to take it back.

Every piece is a placed model with vehicle collision on, so a board rolls up
a ramp's face and grinds along the top of a box. Slabs and platforms to build the ground, banks, quarter
pipes and a wall ride to launch off, boxes, ledges and rails to grind, and the
park furniture round the edges.

## Permissions

| Node | What it allows | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `skateboards.use` | `/skateboard`, and the item that puts a board down | Everyone |
| `skateboards.here` | `/skateboard here`, a board with no item | Operators |
| `skateboards.here.unlimited` | No cooldown or cap on `/skateboard here` | Operators |
| `skateboards.pickup.others` | Picking up a board somebody else put down | Operators |
| `skateboards.ride` | Getting on a board that is already down | Everyone |
| `skateboards.park` | `/skatepark` and its pieces | Operators |
| `skateboards.reload` | `/skateboard reload` | Operators |

Everybody else puts a board down with the item. `/skateboard here` makes one
from nothing, so it is for staff, and it has a cooldown and a cap on how many
boards a player can have standing at once (`spawn-limits` in the config).
Every way of putting a board down, the item included, respects protection: a
WorldGuard region with `rpengine-place` denied, or anything else that keeps
models out of a place, keeps boards out too. Sneak and right-click a board to
pick it up; only the player who put it down can, unless they have
`skateboards.pickup.others`.

`skateboards.use` and `skateboards.ride` are separate because a board left in
a public square is one anybody can right-click. Revoke `ride` and a player can
still put a board down for somebody else; revoke `use` and they cannot get one
at all. Negate both in LuckPerms (or your permissions plugin) to turn the addon
off for a group without uninstalling it.

`skateboards.update` (operators) is told when a newer version is out, if
`update-check` is on.

## Configuration

`plugins/RPESkateboards/config.yml` holds every message the plugin sends, and
these switches:

```yaml
prefix: "&b[Skateboards] &7"

# Land sideways and the rider comes off. Off means a bad landing simply
# carries on.
bail: true

# Whether the board says "Landed it", "Fakie!" and "Bailed!" in chat.
trick-messages: true

# Hand every player a board when they join (needs skateboards.use).
# first-join-only makes it a one-off welcome gift.
give-on-join: false
first-join-only: true

# Nudges you in the console on startup and in chat on join when an update is out.
update-check: true

# /skateboard here: seconds between boards, and how many one player may have
# standing at once (-1 for no cap). skateboards.here.unlimited skips both.
spawn-limits:
  cooldown-seconds: 10
  max-per-player: 2
```

`/skateboard reload` rereads it, including for somebody already on a board.
