# Bones that do something

In Blockbench, a model's parts are grouped into bones. Start a bone's name with
one of these prefixes and it does more than get drawn. They are the same
prefixes Model Engine uses, so a rig you already have, or bought, works without
renaming anything.

| Bone name | What it does |
|---|---|
| `h_head` | Turns to look at whatever its mob is targeting |
| `hi_head` | The same, and every bone under it turns with it |
| `b_wing` | A hitbox of its own, so the model is hit where you aimed |
| `ob_wing` | The same, and the hitbox turns with the bone |
| `p_seat1` | A passenger seat. Right-click it to sit |
| `mount` | Where the driver sits. One per model |
| `tag_name` | Where a name floats |

Heads and seats only work on a [model worn by a mob](/rp-engine/mobs) or a
[custom entity](/rp-engine/entities): a placed statue has nothing to look with
and nobody to carry. Any other bone is an ordinary bone, which is nearly all of
them. A model needs none of these.

## Heads

An `h_` or `hi_` bone turns toward what its mob is fighting, on top of whatever
the animation is doing, so a walk cycle still moves the head.

<Callout type="note" title="It follows the target, not the gaze">
  The server cannot read which way a mob's head is turned, only its body. So a
  head bone looks at the mob's current target, and faces straight ahead when it
  has none.
</Callout>

## Hitboxes

`b_` and `ob_` give a bone a hitbox of its own, so a dragon is hit on the wing
you aimed at instead of inside one box round the whole animal. The hitbox is
sized from the bone and follows the animation. Use `ob_` for bones that rotate
a long way, so the hitbox turns with them.

A hit on a bone is passed on to the mob. The wing has no health of its own: the
mob takes the damage with its own armour, death and loot. You can make some
bones count for more or less:

```yaml
  place:
    hitboxes:
      head: 2.0
      wing: 0.5
```

Each number multiplies the damage of a hit on that bone. It matches the bone's
own name only, not the bones inside it, so a head inside a torso still counts
as a head. (An [animation mask](/rp-engine/animation#masks) works the other way and
includes everything under the bone.)

## Seats

`p_` bones are passenger seats and `mount` is the driver's. Right-click one to
sit; the seat follows the bone, so a seat on a walking mount moves with it.

`mount` must be the whole bone name. A bone called `mountain` is scenery.

## Nametags

`tag_` marks where a name floats. Vanilla puts a name a fixed distance above
the hitbox, which on a large model can be somewhere inside its knee.

The text is the mob's own custom name, and its normal name tag is hidden while
the model is on so there are not two. Taking the model off brings it back.

## Not supported

Procedural bones (tails, chains of segments) and bones that hold an item are
not supported.
