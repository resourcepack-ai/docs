# RP Engine

RP Engine turns a folder on your server into custom content. Items with their
own models, models you can place and sit on, emotes your players wear, sounds,
icons, GUI backdrops, recipes, entities and liquids — written as YAML, built
into a resource pack by the plugin, and served to whoever joins.

It builds the pack itself. There is no zip to make, nowhere to upload it, and
no step where you run a command and then go and find the output.

<Callout type="note" title="Studio is optional">
  You can drive RP Engine entirely from files on disk and never open a browser.
  [Studio](https://studio.resourcepack.ai) makes the art and can push it
  straight to a running server, which is faster — but it is one source of
  content among several, not a requirement. Anything that only worked with
  Studio in the picture would be a bug.
</Callout>

## What it can hold

| Content | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Items](/rp-engine/items) | Sprites or 3D models, wearable as [armour](/rp-engine/armour), carrying real [vanilla stats](/rp-engine/stats) |
| [Actions](/rp-engine/actions) | A closed list of verbs, on nine triggers |
| [Models](/rp-engine/models) | Put down, [sat on](/rp-engine/placement-options), [animated](/rp-engine/animation), [worn by a mob](/rp-engine/mobs) |
| [Emotes](/rp-engine/emotes) | Worn or performed, alone or with a cast |
| [Sounds](/rp-engine/sounds) | Real sound events, with subtitles |
| [Icons](/rp-engine/icons) | Pictures that behave like letters, anywhere text renders |
| [Screens](/rp-engine/screens) and [HUDs](/rp-engine/hud) | GUI backdrops and overlays |
| [Recipes](/rp-engine/recipes) | Crafting, cooking, stonecutting |
| [Entities](/rp-engine/entities) | A real mob wearing a model |
| [Liquids](/rp-engine/liquids) | Pools with rules about who is in them |

## The two ideas worth knowing

**One id for everything, and it is the reference.** `mypack:ruby` is the item,
the model path, and what goes into the stack's persistent data. Nothing is
allocated, there is no numbers file to keep in sync, and nothing can drift out
of step with anything else. **Store ids, never what they resolve to.**

**A bundle is what a player is holding.** A pack is built per bundle rather
than one per server, and a player can hold several stacked on top of each other
— so a lobby pack and a dungeon pack can be different things, and swapping is
instant because the client already has both.
