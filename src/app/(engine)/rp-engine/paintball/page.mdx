# Paintball

An **addon** for [RP Engine](/rp-engine): a plugin that installs into the
engine and adds one thing, rather than a pack you author yourself. Drop it in
beside the engine and it brings its own content with it.

![A paintball match: a paint grenade, the marker and splats on the walls](/images/paintball.webp)

This one is paintball. Hold right-click to fire a paint marker, left-click to
aim down its sights, throw a paint grenade round a corner and leave a mine in a
doorway. One hit splats you and you are back a few seconds later. Play it as
team deathmatch, free for all or capture the flag. One command builds a whole
field to play on.

## Installing

It needs **Spigot or Paper 1.21.11 or newer** (the marker uses item components
1.21.11 added) and **RP Engine 0.2.3 or newer**. On an older engine Paintball
says so in the console and turns itself off.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop it in">
    Put `RPE-paintball.jar` in `plugins/` beside `RPEngine.jar` and start the
    server. Paintball copies its models, sounds, HUD and scoreboard into
    `plugins/RPEngine/content/paintball/` (RP Engine's content folder, which
    the engine builds its resource pack from) and reloads the engine once.
    That folder is yours to edit: an update replaces only the files you have
    not changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a field">
    As an op, stand somewhere flat and run `/pb arena generate main confirm`.
    It builds a field around you and sets the arena up on it. See [Building an
    arena](#building-an-arena) before you do this anywhere you have built.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play">
    `/pb join`, then `/pb start`. Alone is fine: you play against nobody, which
    is a good way to try the marker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you run Multiverse-Core, Paintball loads after it, so an arena in one of its
worlds is found.

## Playing

### The lobby

`/pb join` (or `/pb` on its own) puts you in the lobby: on the field you
generate, a glass box over the middle that looks down on it. Your inventory
and where you were are put away and given back when you leave. The hotbar
holds:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Colour** (the marker) | Right-click for the next colour, and round again to Any. Your paint in a free for all; in a team match, your marker |
| **Practise** (the target) | Down onto the field to practise until the match starts. See [Practising](#practising) |
| **Play for Red** / **Play for Blue** | Pick a side for the next match. Click the same one again to leave it to chance |
| **Scoreboard** | The scoreboard of the last match |
| **Leave** | Back to where you were, with your things |

`/pb team red|blue|auto` does the same as the team items. Sides are kept
within one player of each other, so if too many pick one side, the last to
pick is moved and told. Late joiners go onto the smaller team.

`/pb colour <colour|auto>` does the same as the marker. In a free for all your
colour is your paint (marker, mask, suit and splats). In a team match your
paint stays your team's and only the marker changes. `colour-pick: false` in
the config turns choosing off.

More players than spawns is fine: they share them.

### Practising

While the lobby waits for a match, the target in the hotbar (or
`/pb practice`) takes you down onto the field to try the marker and learn the
map. You get the marker and the grenade, in the colour you chose or your
side's, and everybody down there plays against everybody. A hit sends whoever
it hit straight back to a spawn, the HUD keeps a practice score, and nothing
counts towards anything. The feather in your last slot, or `/pb practice`
again, takes you back up to the lobby.

When the match starts, everybody practising is put at their spawn like
everybody else and the paint comes off the walls. `practice: false` in the
config, or for one arena with `/pb arena set`, turns practising off.

### In a match

Every match opens with a three-second countdown, frozen in place, then a
whistle. You carry a marker, a paint grenade, a paint mine, a mask and a suit
in your team's colour.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Right-click** (hold) | Fire. About seven balls a second, forty to a hopper |
| **Left-click** | Up to the sights, or back down. Zoomed in, slower on your feet, and no spread at all |
| **Q** | Reload. The marker also reloads by itself when the hopper runs dry |
| **Right-click** the grenade | Throw it |
| **Right-click** the ground with the mine | Set it |
| **F** | The scoreboard |
| **Tab** | The scoreboard in the player list: the score and the clock on top, and a tile for every player with their kills and deaths |

**Every ball lands on the crosshair.** Balls drop and slow as they fly, but
are aimed so they arrive where you are looking. From the hip they stray a
little (the crosshair's ticks show how much): more on the move, in the air or
sprinting, half as much crouched. Down the sights there is no spread.
Changing slot, sprinting or reloading takes you off the sights.

**The grenade** goes off a second and a half after it lands and splats every
enemy within three and a half blocks that it can see, so a wall is cover. It
recharges for fifteen seconds after a throw (`grenade.reset-on-respawn: true`
makes a respawn bring it straight back).

**The mine** is one per match. It arms a second and a half after you set it,
and the first enemy to step on it splats every enemy within three blocks that
it can see. Your own team can walk over it.

**One hit splats you.** For three seconds the camera circles whoever got you,
then you come back at one of your team's spawns, safe from hits for a second
and a half. A server can also give each spawn a safe zone
(`spawn-protection-radius`): you stay safe until you leave it or fire, and the
other side cannot set a mine in it. There is no friendly fire, and enemy name
tags are hidden.

A pling sounds at one minute and thirty seconds left, and the last seconds
count down in the middle of the screen. The match ends when the clock runs out
(three minutes) or when a team reaches the kill limit, if one is set. Then the
result, the scoreboard, and back to the lobby. Splats are displays, not
blocks, so the arena is never changed and is clean for the next match.

### What you see

There is not a title or a boss bar in the game. **The whole screen is one
HUD**:

- **Top centre**, the score strip: both teams' kills and the clock.
- **Top left**, the kill feed: the last three splats, who by, and with what.
- **Bottom right**, your weapon: the grenade's card, filling as it recharges,
  and the ammo panel with its bar.
- **The crosshair**, with its spread ticks from the hip and a hit marker when
  you land one.
- **The middle**, which carries every moment of a match: the countdown ring,
  GO!, the paint on your screen when you are splatted and the ring counting
  you back in, SPLATTED and who when you get somebody, the time left as the
  clock runs down, and the result on its band with the final score.

**The scoreboard** (F, `/pb scoreboard`, or the lobby's item) is a dialog with
both teams' tables, the top five of each, best first: each player's face,
kills, deaths and accuracy.

**The player list** (Tab) is a scoreboard too. Under the name, the mode, the
map, the score and the clock, every player has a tile in their colour with
their name and their kills and deaths: a column a team, red on the left, or in
a free for all everybody best first with their place. Your own tile is lit,
and the name of somebody who is out is greyed. In the lobby it shows who is
waiting, in the colour of the side they picked. On Paper the players in the
match are taken off the list under the tiles, which already show them; Spigot
has no way to, so there they stay. `tab.scoreboard: false` keeps the game's own
list instead.

### While you are playing

In the lobby and in a match alike, you take no damage and get no hungrier,
and you cannot build, break, open doors or chests, pick anything up, or move
or drop what you carry. Commands are limited to `/pb`, `/msg`, `/tell`, `/w`,
`/r`, `/reply`, `/me` and `/list`, unless you have
`paintball.bypass.commands`. Walking out of the arena's bounds during a match
puts you back where you last stood.

`/pb leave` always works. Disconnecting or being teleported to another world
counts as leaving. If the server goes down mid-match, everybody gets their
things back the next time they join.

## Game modes

Every arena plays one of three modes, `teams` unless it says otherwise.

| Mode | How it plays |
|---|---|
| **Team deathmatch** (`teams`) | Red against blue. Most splats when the clock runs out wins, or the first team to the kill limit if there is one |
| **Free for all** (`ffa`) | Everybody against everybody, each in a paint colour of their own. First to 15 splats, or the most when the clock runs out, wins |
| **Capture the flag** (`ctf`) | Red against blue, for each other's flag. First team to 3 captures, or the most when the clock runs out, wins |

Choose an arena's mode with `/pb arena set <arena> mode ffa`, or play another
one for a single match with `/pb start <arena> ctf`. The lobby says which mode
is next.

`max-players` caps how many people an arena takes, its lobby and its match
together, so one arena can be a four-player free for all and another a 5v5
(`/pb arena set <arena> max-players 4`). Anybody past it is told the arena is
full. Bots are not counted, and 0 is no limit.

### Free for all

Every player gets one of eight paint colours (red, blue, green, orange, yellow,
purple, pink and cyan), unless they chose one. More players than colours share
them. Everybody else is an enemy, and nobody's name tag shows. You respawn at
whichever spawn is furthest from everybody still in.

The HUD shows your kills and the leader's either side of the clock, with your
place under them. A tie on kills goes to whoever was splatted less; a tie on
both is a draw. The scoreboard is one table of everybody.

### Capture the flag

Each team has a flag at its base. Walk into the other team's flag to take it.
While you carry it you glow, walk a little slower, and cannot aim down the
sights, throw your grenade or set your mine. Bring it to your own flag while
yours is at home and your team scores a capture.

If the carrier is splatted, the flag drops where they fell. An enemy can pick
it up again. A teammate of the flag's who touches it sends it home, and
otherwise it goes home on its own after twenty seconds. The HUD shows each
flag's state beside its team's captures: at home, taken or dropped. The middle
of the screen and chat say when a flag is taken, dropped, returned or
captured.

A generated field has both flags in place. On an arena you set up by hand,
stand where each one goes and run `/pb arena setflag <arena> red` and `blue`.

## Running matches

Matches do not start on their own: somebody with `paintball.start` runs
`/pb start`.

- **`/pb start`** starts a match now on the arena you are in, joining you
  first if you are not in (so it works alone). `/pb start <arena>` names one,
  and `/pb start <arena> <teams|ffa|ctf>` plays that mode this once.
- **`/pb stop`** ends a match the ordinary way (result, scoreboard, lobby). In
  the lobby it sends everybody home.

**`/pb as <player> <subcommand>`** runs a subcommand as somebody else, for
NPCs and command blocks: an NPC that runs `/pb as <player> join` for whoever
clicks it is a join button. It is checked against **the player's**
permissions, not the sender's.

### Bots

Computer players can fill a match up. They are off until you turn them on in
`config.yml`, or for one arena with `/pb arena set`:

- **`bots.fill`**: fill each side up to this many in a team match, or everybody
  up to this many in a free for all.
- **`bots.even-teams`**: in a team match, add a bot for every extra person the
  other side has, so three against one becomes three against three.

Bots are in the kill feed, the scoreboard and the score like anybody else.
They shoot the nearest enemy they can see after a short reaction time
(`bots.reaction-ticks`), with some spread (`bots.spread-degrees`). In capture
the flag half of them attack and half guard. They do not throw grenades or set
mines, and never count towards stats.

A person who joins takes a bot's place, and when the last person leaves the
match ends. Bots are mannequins, so they need Minecraft 1.21.9 or newer.

## Building an arena

An arena is a waiting spot, at least one spawn for each team, and bounds: the
box a player cannot walk out of during a match. Arenas are kept in
`plugins/RPEPaintball/arenas.yml`. An arena id is lower-case letters, digits,
`-` and `_`, up to 32 of them; its display name can be anything.

### Generate a field

```
/pb arena generate <id> confirm [name]
```

Builds a speedball field centred on you and sets the arena up on it, ready to
play: a floor, a netted fence, a base and flag at each end, mirrored bunkers,
and a glass box over the middle to wait in. Without `confirm` it only says
what it is about to do.

<Callout type="warning" title="It replaces every block in a large box">
  The field is 31 blocks across and 57 long. Inside that it clears everything
  from your feet to eleven blocks up, lays the floor and a foundation in the
  two layers below, and builds the waiting box above the middle, up to
  seventeen blocks above your feet. Run it in an
  empty world, or somewhere you do not mind losing. A flat world is best.
</Callout>

Running it again with the same id rebuilds the field where you now stand. From
the console, name the place outright:

```
/pb arena generate <id> at <world> <x> <y> <z> [name]
```

### Set one up by hand

To play on a map you built yourself, stand in the right places and run:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    `/pb arena create <id> [name]`, in the world the arena is in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where players wait">
    `/pb arena setwaiting <id>` where you stand, facing the way you face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawns">
    `/pb arena addspawn <id> red`, and again for `blue`, up to five a team
    (past five the oldest goes). With no colour, `/pb arena addspawn <id>` adds
    a spawn for anybody (up to ten), used by free for all and by any team with
    no spawns of its own, so a free-for-all arena needs no team spawns.
    `/pb arena clearspawns <id> [red|blue]` clears them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bounds">
    `/pb arena wand` hands you the bounds wand. Left-click a block for one
    corner, right-click another for the opposite corner, then
    `/pb arena setbounds <id>`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    `/pb arena enable <id>`. If anything is missing it says what.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/pb arena list` shows every arena, its world, and whether it is open, ready,
or what it still needs.

### Managing arenas

`/pb join` with no arena named goes to the first open one.
`/pb arena disable <id>` closes an arena for maintenance: nobody can join or
start it, its lobby is sent home, and a match on it ends as `/pb stop` would.
Staff can still `tp` there and change it. `/pb arena delete <id>` does the
same and forgets the arena, leaving the blocks where they are. Spawns and
bounds cannot be changed during a match: `/pb stop <id>` first.

### Settings per arena

Every number in [the configuration](#configuration), and each team's marker,
is the server's default. An arena can set any of them for itself:

```
/pb arena set <id> <option> <value>
```

```
/pb arena set main match-seconds 300
/pb arena set main kill-limit 25
/pb arena set main marker.hopper 60
/pb arena set main teams.red.marker gold
/pb arena set main hud.time-warnings 60,30,10
```

An option is its path in `config.yml` (tab-completion lists them; `ui`,
`messages` and `update-check` are server-wide only). `default` puts one back to
`config.yml`'s value, and `/pb arena set <id>` lists what the arena overrides.
They are saved under `settings:` in `arenas.yml`. Changes apply at once, so
change the clock and limits between matches.

## Permissions

One node per command, so a referee can start and stop matches without
touching arenas. `paintball.play` and `paintball.admin` are shorthand that
grant the nodes under them. `/pb leave` needs no permission.

| Node | What it allows | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `paintball.play` | Everything a player does: join, pick a team and a colour, the scoreboard, practising. Grants the five below | Everyone |
| `paintball.join` | `/pb join`: into an arena's lobby | Everyone |
| `paintball.team` | `/pb team`, and the red and blue items in the lobby's hotbar | Everyone |
| `paintball.colour` | `/pb colour`, and the marker in the lobby's hotbar, which picks your colour | Everyone |
| `paintball.scoreboard` | `/pb scoreboard`, F in a match or its lobby, and the lobby's scoreboard item | Everyone |
| `paintball.practice` | `/pb practice`, and the target in the lobby's hotbar, which takes you down onto the field to practise | Everyone |
| `paintball.admin` | Every staff node: start, stop, reload, `/pb as`, arenas, the command bypass. Grants the eight below | Operators |
| `paintball.start` | `/pb start`: start a match now, in any mode (it joins you first if you are not in) | Operators |
| `paintball.stop` | `/pb stop`: end a match, or empty a lobby | Operators |
| `paintball.reload` | `/pb reload`: reread `config.yml` and `arenas.yml` | Operators |
| `paintball.as` | `/pb as <player> ...`: run a subcommand as somebody else (for NPCs and console). Checked against **their** permissions | Operators |
| `paintball.arena` | Arena setup: `list`, `create`, `setwaiting`, `addspawn`, `clearspawns`, `setflag`, `wand` and `setbounds`, `enable`, `disable`, `tp`, `name`, `set` | Operators |
| `paintball.arena.generate` | `/pb arena generate`: build a whole field, replacing every block in a large box | Operators |
| `paintball.arena.delete` | `/pb arena delete`: remove an arena, ending its match | Operators |
| `paintball.bypass.commands` | Run any command during a match, not only the few a player may | Operators |
| `paintball.update` | Be told when a newer Paintball is on SpigotMC. Not part of `paintball.admin` | Operators |

## Configuration

`plugins/RPEPaintball/config.yml`. The numbers, which are the server's
defaults: [an arena can set any of them for itself](#settings-per-arena).

```yaml
match-seconds: 180
# End early when a team reaches this many kills. 0 plays the whole clock.
kill-limit: 0
freeze-seconds: 3
respawn-seconds: 3
# While you're out, the camera circles whoever splatted you. false leaves you a
# free spectator where you fell.
killer-camera: true
# A player just back at a spawn can't be splatted for this long.
spawn-protection-seconds: 1.5
# And, with a radius, for as long as they stay inside this many blocks of the
# spawn they came in at, however long that is: a circle round it, or a square.
# Firing, throwing a grenade or setting a mine ends it, so nobody shoots out of
# a safe zone, and nobody can set a mine in the other side's. The border is
# drawn for whoever is inside. 0 for none.
spawn-protection-radius: 0
spawn-protection-shape: circle
# How long the final scoreboard stays up before everybody is back in the lobby.
end-seconds: 12
# While they wait for a match, players can go down onto the field to practise
# (the target in the lobby's hotbar, or /pb practice): the marker and the
# grenade, everybody down there against everybody, a hit sends you back to a
# spawn, and nothing counts. The match starting puts everybody at their spawn.
practice: true

marker:
  # Ticks between shots while the trigger is held (3 = about seven a second).
  fire-interval-ticks: 3
  hopper: 40
  reload-seconds: 1.6
  # Blocks per tick as the ball leaves the barrel, and what pulls it down.
  speed: 2.4
  gravity: 0.022
  drag: 0.992
  spread-degrees: 0.7
  moving-spread-degrees: 2.4
  # Left click aims down the sights: no spread at all, and slower on your feet.
  aim-spread-degrees: 0.0
  aim-slowdown: 0.55
  # How far out the crosshair finds what you are aiming at.
  range: 96
  lifetime-ticks: 60

grenade:
  # Greyed out for this long after a throw. 0 for no wait at all.
  cooldown-seconds: 15
  # true: being splatted brings it straight back.
  reset-on-respawn: false
  fuse-ticks: 30
  throw-speed: 1.1
  radius: 3.5

mine:
  # How many mines each player can set in a match. 0 for none.
  per-match: 1
  # How long after it is set before it can go off.
  arm-seconds: 1.5
  # How far it splats enemies it can see when it goes off.
  radius: 3.0

paint:
  # The most splats one match keeps on the walls; the oldest go first.
  max-decals: 1500

# Which marker each team carries: ember, tide, toxic, sunset or gold.
teams:
  red:
    marker: ember
  blue:
    marker: tide

hud:
  kill-feed-seconds: 8
  kill-banner-seconds: 2.5
  hit-marker-ticks: 6
  # Seconds left at which a pling sounds and the time left is shown.
  time-warnings: [60, 30, 10, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1]

tab:
  # The player list (Tab) as the match's scoreboard: a tile for every player
  # with their name, kills and deaths. false leaves the game's own list.
  scoreboard: true
  # On Paper, the match's players are taken off the list under the tiles.
  hide-players: true

nametags:
  # The names over players' heads. The game's own show through walls; with this
  # on they are text that blocks in front of it hide, like anything else, and
  # the game's own are hidden for the match even when a tab or name tag plugin
  # draws them.
  hide-behind-blocks: false
  # Whether you see the other team's names too, not only your own team's. With
  # hide-behind-blocks off, that means through walls as well.
  enemies: false
  # How a name reads, in MiniMessage, with hide-behind-blocks on. {player} is
  # the name and {team_colour} its paint colour (#FF4655 or #2E9BFF in a team
  # match), which colours what follows it whether or not it is in <>.
  format: "<{team_colour}>{player}"

# What an arena plays unless it sets its own: teams, ffa or ctf.
mode: teams

# The most people one arena takes, its lobby and its match together; anybody
# after that is told it is full. Bots are not counted. 0 for no limit.
max-players: 0

ffa:
  # The paint colours handed out, in this order, one to a player.
  colours: [red, blue, green, orange, yellow, purple, pink, cyan]
  # The marker each colour carries.
  markers:
    red: ember
    blue: tide
    green: toxic
    orange: sunset
    yellow: gold
    purple: violet
    pink: bubblegum
    cyan: frost

# Whether players choose their colour (/pb colour, or the lobby's marker) out
# of ffa.colours: their paint in a free for all, their marker in a team match.
colour-pick: true

bots:
  # Each side filled up to this many in a team match, everybody up to this
  # many in a free for all. 0 for none.
  fill: 0
  # A bot for every person the other side has more.
  even-teams: false
  # How far a shot strays, in degrees, and how long before they fire at
  # somebody who comes into view, in ticks.
  spread-degrees: 4.0
  reaction-ticks: 12
  names: [Splotch, Drip, Smudge, Blot, Streak, Dab, Speckle, Spritz, Gloop, Swatch]

ctf:
  # Captures to win. The team with more when the clock runs out wins.
  captures: 3
  # A dropped flag goes home on its own after this long.
  flag-return-seconds: 20
  # Whether your own flag has to be at home for you to score with theirs.
  need-own-flag: true
  # How fast the carrier walks, as a share of their normal speed.
  carrier-speed: 0.85
  # Whether the carrier glows, seen through walls by everybody.
  carrier-glows: true
  # Whether the carrier can throw their grenade and set their mine.
  carrier-can-throw: false
  # How close you have to come to a flag to take it, return it or score.
  flag-reach: 1.3

# Any option above, changed for one mode's matches only.
modes:
  teams: {}
  ffa:
    match-seconds: 240
    kill-limit: 15
    respawn-seconds: 2
  ctf:
    match-seconds: 480
    respawn-seconds: 5

update-check: true
```

`modes.<mode>` values apply to that mode's matches only, and an arena's own
settings apply on top of both (`/pb arena set <arena> modes.ctf.respawn-seconds 6`).

**Every word a player reads** is under `messages:` in the same file, in
[MiniMessage](https://docs.advntr.dev/minimessage/format.html): the HUD's, the
scoreboard's (its headings and its Close button), the player list's, and the
names of the teams, the paint colours and the markers (`team-red`,
`colour-purple`, `marker-ember`...). The big words in the middle of the HUD
(GO!, SPLATTED BY, VICTORY...) can use only A to Z, digits, `!`, `-`, `'` and
spaces.

**To replace the HUD or a scoreboard** with your own RP Engine HUD or Dialog,
point `ui.hud`, `ui.hud-ffa`, `ui.scoreboard`, `ui.scoreboard-ffa` or
`ui.scoreboard-ctf` at its id. It has to show the same values.

`/pb reload` rereads the file, and `arenas.yml` too unless somebody is playing.
An update adds new options to your file with their defaults and leaves yours
alone.

## Placeholders

With [PlaceholderAPI](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/placeholderapi.6245/)
on the server, Paintball registers `%paintball_...%` placeholders for
scoreboards, holograms and anything else that reads them. Every match that
ends, by the clock, the kill limit or `/pb stop`, is added to each player's
totals, which are kept in `plugins/RPEPaintball/stats.json`. A player who
leaves before the end keeps their kills and deaths but gets no win, loss or
match; a draw is neither a win nor a loss.

| Placeholder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `%paintball_arena_status_<arena>%` | Maintenance (closed), Not Configured, Ready, Starting, In Game or Ending |
| `%paintball_arena_players_<arena>%` | How many are in its lobby or match |
| `%paintball_arena_max_<arena>%` | How many it takes (`max-players`), 0 for no limit |
| `%paintball_arena_plays_<arena>%` | How many matches it has had |
| `%paintball_arena_name_<arena>%` | Its display name |
| `%paintball_arena_red_<arena>%`, `%paintball_arena_blue_<arena>%` | Each team's score in its current or last match: kills, or in capture the flag captures |
| `%paintball_arena_time_<arena>%` | Its match clock |
| `%paintball_arena_mode_<arena>%` | The mode being played, or the one it plays next |
| `%paintball_arena_leader_<arena>%` | Who is ahead: a team, or in a free for all a player |
| `%paintball_player_<stat>%` | The player's own total |
| `%paintball_player_arena%`, `%paintball_player_team%` | Where the player is playing, and for whom |
| `%paintball_leaderboard_<stat>_<n>%` | Who is n-th for that stat |
| `%paintball_leaderboard_<stat>_<n>_value%` | Their number |

A `<stat>` is `kills`, `deaths`, `wins` (or `won`), `losses` (or `lost`),
`matches` (or `played`), `shots`, `hits`, `captures`, `accuracy` or `kd`. A
win in a free for all is coming first.

The status words are under `messages:` (`status-maintenance` ... `status-ending`).

## Update check

With `update-check: true`, Paintball checks SpigotMC once at startup and, if
you are behind, tells the console and every player with `paintball.update`
(operators) with a link. Set it to `false` to turn that off.
