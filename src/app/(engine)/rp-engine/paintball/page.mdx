# Paintball

An **addon** for [RP Engine](/rp-engine): a plugin that installs into the
engine and adds one thing, rather than a pack you author yourself. Drop it in
beside the engine and it brings its own content with it.

![A paintball match: a paint grenade, the marker and splats on the walls](/images/paintball.webp)

This one is paintball, red against blue. Hold right-click to fire a paint
marker, left-click to aim down its sights, and throw a paint grenade round a
corner. One hit splats you, you are back three seconds later, and the team with
more kills when the clock runs out wins. Everything on screen during a match is
drawn by a full-screen HUD, and one command builds a whole field to play on.

## Installing

It needs:

- **Spigot or Paper, 1.21.11 or newer.** The marker is held with item
  components that 1.21.11 added (hold to fire, no swing when you click, no
  reach to punch with).
- **RP Engine 0.2.3 or newer.** Paintball checks the engine's version as it
  starts, and on anything older says so in the console and disables itself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop it in">
    Put `RPE-paintball.jar` in `plugins/` beside `RPEngine.jar` and start the
    server. Its content (the marker, mask, grenade and paint models, the
    sounds, the HUD and the scoreboard) installs itself into
    `plugins/RPEngine/content/paintball/` on first boot, and the engine reloads
    once so the resource pack it serves carries all of it. That folder is
    yours: edit anything in it, and a new version of the plugin updates only
    the files you have not changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a field">
    As an op, stand somewhere flat and run `/pb arena generate main confirm`.
    It builds a field around you and sets the arena up on it. See [Building an
    arena](#building-an-arena) before you do this anywhere you have built.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play">
    `/pb join`, then `/pb start`. Alone is fine: you play against nobody, which
    is a good way to try the marker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you run Multiverse-Core, Paintball loads after it, so an arena in one of its
worlds is found.

## Playing

### The lobby

`/pb join` (or `/pb` on its own) puts you in the lobby: on the field you
generate, a glass box over the middle that looks down on it. Your inventory
and where you were are put away and given back when you leave. The hotbar
holds:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play for Red** / **Play for Blue** | Pick a side for the next match. Click the same one again to leave it to chance |
| **Scoreboard** | The scoreboard of the last match |
| **Leave** | Back to where you were, with your things |

`/pb team red|blue|auto` does the same as the two team items. Sides are kept
within one player of each other: if too many people pick one, whoever picked
it last plays for the other and is told so. Anybody who did not pick fills in
where they are needed. Somebody who joins once a match is under way goes
straight onto the smaller team.

The field has five spawns a side. That is not a cap: more players simply
share them.

### In a match

Every match opens with a three-second countdown, frozen in place, then a
whistle. You carry a marker, a paint grenade, a mask and a suit in your team's
colour.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Right-click** (hold) | Fire. About seven balls a second, forty to a hopper |
| **Left-click** | Up to the sights, or back down. Zoomed in, slower on your feet, and no spread at all |
| **Q** | Reload. The marker also reloads by itself when the hopper runs dry |
| **Right-click** the grenade | Throw it |
| **F** | The scoreboard |
| **Tab** | The player list: the score and the clock on top, each player's kills and deaths |

**Every ball lands on the crosshair.** It drops and slows as it flies, and it
is fired so that, drop and all, it arrives where you are looking. From the hip
it strays a little, by the spread the crosshair's ticks show: a little standing
still, more on the move, in the air or sprinting, and half of either crouched.
Down the sights there is none. Changing slot, sprinting or reloading brings
the sights down.

**The grenade** goes off a second and a half after it lands and splats every
enemy within three and a half blocks that it can see: a wall between you and
it is cover. It stays in your hotbar, greyed out for fifteen seconds after a
throw, and being splatted does not bring it back any sooner.

**One hit splats you.** You watch as a spectator for three seconds, then come
back at one of your team's spawns, protected for a second and a half: a ball
that hits you in that time does nothing. There is no friendly fire. Enemy name
tags are hidden, so you find people by looking for them.

A pling sounds with a minute and with thirty seconds left, and the time left
shows in the middle of the screen; for the last ten seconds it is the number
itself, at ten and then from five down. The match ends when the clock runs out
(three minutes) or, if the server sets one, when a team reaches the kill limit. The result has the screen to itself
for three seconds, then everybody gets the scoreboard, and a few seconds later
it is back to the lobby for another. The paint comes off the walls between
matches. It was never blocks: every splat is a display, so the arena itself is
never changed.

### What you see

There is not a title or a boss bar in the game. **The whole screen is one
HUD**:

- **Top centre**, the score strip: both teams' kills and the clock.
- **Top left**, the kill feed: the last three splats, who by, and with what.
- **Bottom right**, your weapon: the grenade's card, filling as it recharges,
  and the ammo panel with its bar.
- **The crosshair**, with its spread ticks from the hip and a hit marker when
  you land one.
- **The middle**, which carries every moment of a match: the countdown ring,
  GO!, the paint on your screen when you are splatted and the ring counting
  you back in, SPLATTED and who when you get somebody, the time left as the
  clock runs down, and the result on its band with the final score.

**The scoreboard** (F, `/pb scoreboard`, or the lobby's item) is a dialog with
both teams' tables, the top five of each, best first: each player's face,
kills, deaths and accuracy.

### While you are playing

In the lobby and in a match alike, you take no damage and get no hungrier,
and you cannot build, break, open doors or chests, pick anything up, or move
or drop what you carry. Commands are limited to `/pb`, `/msg`, `/tell`, `/w`,
`/r`, `/reply`, `/me` and `/list`, unless you have
`paintball.bypass.commands`. Walking out of the arena's bounds during a match
puts you back where you last stood.

`/pb leave` always works and needs no permission, so nobody can be stuck in a
match. Disconnecting, or being teleported to another world by something else,
counts as leaving. If the server goes down mid-match, everybody gets their
things back the next time they join.

## Running matches

Matches do not start on their own: somebody with `paintball.start` runs
`/pb start`. Players who can start a match are told so when they join the
lobby; everybody else is told the lobby is waiting for an op.

- **`/pb start`** starts a match now, on the arena you are in. If you are not
  in yet it joins you first, which is why it works alone. `/pb start <arena>`
  names one.
- **`/pb stop`** during a match ends it the ordinary way: the result, the
  scoreboard, then back to the lobby. In the lobby it sends everybody home.

**`/pb as <player> <subcommand>`** runs a subcommand as somebody else, for
NPCs, command blocks and anything else that can only run console commands:
an NPC that runs `/pb as <player> join` for whoever clicks it is a join button. What the player runs
is checked against **their** permissions, not the sender's.

## Building an arena

An arena is a waiting spot, at least one spawn for each team, and bounds: the
box a player cannot walk out of during a match. Arenas are kept in
`plugins/RPEPaintball/arenas.yml`. An arena id is lower-case letters, digits,
`-` and `_`, up to 32 of them; its display name can be anything.

### Generate a field

```
/pb arena generate <id> confirm [name]
```

Builds a speedball field centred on you and sets the arena up on it, open
straight away: a floor paint shows up on, a netted fence, a base at each end,
inflatable-style bunkers placed with point symmetry so both teams play the same
field, and a glass box over the middle to wait in. Without `confirm` it only
says what it is about to do.

<Callout type="warning" title="It replaces every block in a large box">
  The field is 31 blocks across and 57 long. Inside that it clears everything
  from your feet to eleven blocks up, lays the floor and a foundation in the
  two layers below, and builds the waiting box above the middle, up to
  seventeen blocks above your feet. Run it in an
  empty world, or somewhere you do not mind losing. A flat world is best.
</Callout>

Running it again with the same id rebuilds the field where you now stand. From
the console, name the place outright:

```
/pb arena generate <id> at <world> <x> <y> <z> [name]
```

### Set one up by hand

To play on a map you built yourself, stand in the right places and run:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    `/pb arena create <id> [name]`, in the world the arena is in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where players wait">
    `/pb arena setwaiting <id>` where you stand, facing the way you face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawns">
    `/pb arena addspawn <id> red`, and again for `blue`. Up to five a team;
    past five the oldest goes. `/pb arena clearspawns <id> <red|blue>` starts
    a team's over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bounds">
    `/pb arena wand` hands you the bounds wand. Left-click a block for one
    corner, right-click another for the opposite corner, then
    `/pb arena setbounds <id>`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    `/pb arena enable <id>`. If anything is missing it says what.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`/pb arena list` shows every arena, its world, and whether it is open, ready,
or what it still needs.

### Managing arenas

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/pb arena enable <id>` | Opens it. `/pb join` with no arena named goes to the first open one |
| `/pb arena disable <id>` | Closes it until `enable` opens it again. See below |
| `/pb arena tp <id>` | Takes you to its waiting spot |
| `/pb arena name <id> <name>` | Renames it. The id stays the same |
| `/pb arena delete <id>` | Removes it, ending any match on it and sending its players home. The blocks are left where they are |

A closed arena cannot be joined or started by anybody, however they ask:
`/pb join <id>`, `/pb start <id>` and `/pb as` all say it is closed. Closing
one sends its lobby home, and a match being played on it ends the way
`/pb stop` ends one (the result, the scoreboard), after which everybody goes
home rather than back to the lobby. Deleting an arena does the same. Staff can
still `tp` to a closed arena and change it.

Spawns and bounds cannot be changed while a match is being played on an arena:
`/pb stop <id>` first.

## Commands

`/paintball`, or `/pb` for short. `/pb help` and tab-completion only offer
what you may run.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/pb join [arena]` | Into the lobby. `/pb` on its own does the same |
| `/pb team <red\|blue\|auto>` | The side you would like, from the next match |
| `/pb leave` | Out, with your inventory and position back |
| `/pb scoreboard` | The match scoreboard (F in a match or its lobby) |
| `/pb start [arena]` | Start a match now, joining you first if you are not in |
| `/pb stop [arena]` | End the match, or in the lobby, send everybody home |
| `/pb as <player> <subcommand>` | Run a subcommand as somebody else |
| `/pb reload` | Reread `config.yml` and `arenas.yml` |
| `/pb arena list` | Every arena and what it still needs |
| `/pb arena generate <id> confirm [name]` | Build a field where you stand |
| `/pb arena generate <id> at <world> <x> <y> <z> [name]` | The same, from the console |
| `/pb arena create <id> [name]` | A new, empty arena in your world |
| `/pb arena setwaiting <id>` | Its waiting spot, where you stand |
| `/pb arena addspawn <id> <red\|blue>` | A team spawn, where you stand |
| `/pb arena clearspawns <id> <red\|blue>` | Remove a team's spawns |
| `/pb arena wand` | The bounds wand |
| `/pb arena setbounds <id>` | Its bounds, from the two corners you marked |
| `/pb arena enable <id>` / `disable <id>` | Open or close it |
| `/pb arena tp <id>` | Go to its waiting spot |
| `/pb arena name <id> <name>` | Rename it |
| `/pb arena delete <id>` | Remove it |

## Permissions

One node per command, so you can hand out exactly what somebody needs: a
referee who can start and stop matches but not touch arenas, say.
`paintball.play` and `paintball.admin` are shorthand: each grants every node
listed under it, and nothing checks them directly. `/pb leave` needs no
permission at all.

| Node | What it allows | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `paintball.play` | Everything a player does: join, pick a team, the scoreboard. Grants the three below | Everyone |
| `paintball.join` | `/pb join`: into an arena's lobby | Everyone |
| `paintball.team` | `/pb team`, and the red and blue items in the lobby's hotbar | Everyone |
| `paintball.scoreboard` | `/pb scoreboard`, F in a match or its lobby, and the lobby's scoreboard item | Everyone |
| `paintball.admin` | Every staff node: start, stop, reload, `/pb as`, arenas, the command bypass. Grants the eight below | Operators |
| `paintball.start` | `/pb start`: start a match now (it joins you first if you are not in) | Operators |
| `paintball.stop` | `/pb stop`: end a match, or empty a lobby | Operators |
| `paintball.reload` | `/pb reload`: reread `config.yml` and `arenas.yml` | Operators |
| `paintball.as` | `/pb as <player> ...`: run a subcommand as somebody else (for NPCs and console). Checked against **their** permissions | Operators |
| `paintball.arena` | Arena setup: `list`, `create`, `setwaiting`, `addspawn`, `clearspawns`, `wand` and `setbounds`, `enable`, `disable`, `tp`, `name` | Operators |
| `paintball.arena.generate` | `/pb arena generate`: build a whole field, replacing every block in a large box | Operators |
| `paintball.arena.delete` | `/pb arena delete`: remove an arena, ending its match | Operators |
| `paintball.bypass.commands` | Run any command during a match, not only the few a player may | Operators |
| `paintball.update` | Be told when a newer Paintball is on SpigotMC. Not part of `paintball.admin` | Operators |

## Configuration

`plugins/RPEPaintball/config.yml`. The numbers:

```yaml
match-seconds: 180
# End early when a team reaches this many kills. 0 plays the whole clock.
kill-limit: 0
freeze-seconds: 3
respawn-seconds: 3
spawn-protection-seconds: 1.5
# How long the final scoreboard stays up before everybody is back in the lobby.
end-seconds: 12

marker:
  # Ticks between shots while the trigger is held (3 = about seven a second).
  fire-interval-ticks: 3
  hopper: 40
  reload-seconds: 1.6
  # Blocks per tick as the ball leaves the barrel, and what pulls it down.
  speed: 2.4
  gravity: 0.022
  drag: 0.992
  spread-degrees: 0.7
  moving-spread-degrees: 2.4
  # Left click aims down the sights: no spread at all, and slower on your feet.
  aim-spread-degrees: 0.0
  aim-slowdown: 0.55
  # How far out the crosshair finds what you are aiming at.
  range: 96
  lifetime-ticks: 60

grenade:
  # Greyed out for this long after a throw. 0 for no wait at all.
  cooldown-seconds: 15
  fuse-ticks: 30
  throw-speed: 1.1
  radius: 3.5

paint:
  # The most splats one match keeps on the walls; the oldest go first.
  max-decals: 1500

# Which marker each team carries: ember, tide, toxic, sunset or gold.
teams:
  red:
    marker: ember
  blue:
    marker: tide

hud:
  kill-feed-seconds: 8
  kill-banner-seconds: 2.5
  hit-marker-ticks: 6
  # Seconds left at which a pling sounds and the time left is shown.
  time-warnings: [60, 30, 10, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1]

update-check: true
```

**Every word a player reads** is under `messages:` in the same file, in
[MiniMessage](https://docs.advntr.dev/minimessage/format.html): chat, the
lobby's hotbar, the player list, the scoreboard's heading and the HUD's text.
The big words in the middle of the HUD (YOU'RE TEAM, GO!, SPLATTED BY,
VICTORY...) are drawn in the HUD's own lettering, which has the letters A to Z,
the digits, `!`, `-`, `'` and spaces: anything else in them is left out. The
scoreboard's column headings are part of its picture, in the content folder.

**The HUD and the scoreboard are RP Engine content** like any other, so
`ui.hud` and `ui.scoreboard` can point at a HUD or dialog of your own to
replace either. A replacement has to show the same values.

`/pb reload` rereads the file, and `arenas.yml` too unless somebody is playing.
Options a newer version adds are written into your existing file when the
server starts, with their defaults, and nothing already there is touched.

## Update check

With `update-check: true`, Paintball asks SpigotMC for its current version a
few seconds after the server starts and every six hours after that. While you
are behind, it says so in the console, and to every player with
`paintball.update` (operators, by default) on each check and when they join,
with a link to the SpigotMC page. Set it to `false` to turn that off.
