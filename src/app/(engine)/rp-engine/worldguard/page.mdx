# WorldGuard

With WorldGuard installed, RP Engine adds two region flags, so you can keep
custom content out of places you already protect: a spawn where nobody may put
models down, an arena where custom items do nothing.

```
/rg flag spawn rpengine-place deny
/rg flag arena rpengine-use deny
```

| Flag | Covers |
|---|---|
| `rpengine-place` | Putting a model down, and breaking one |
| `rpengine-use` | Using a custom item |

Both allow by default, so installing WorldGuard changes nothing until you set
one. Players with WorldGuard's region bypass are not stopped.

<Callout type="note" title="If something goes wrong, it allows">
  If a flag failed to register or WorldGuard throws an error, the action is
  allowed. A broken hook never locks your players out of their content
  everywhere.
</Callout>

## Placement already respects protection

Even without the flags, placing a model asks the server whether that player
could have placed a block there. So spawn protection and most claim plugins
already apply, with nothing to configure.

The flags are for treating models and items differently from blocks: a region
where building is fine but statues are not.

## Other protection plugins

The flags work by cancelling RP Engine's `ModelPlaceEvent`,
`ModelBreakEvent` and `ItemUseEvent`. Any other plugin, such as a claims
plugin or your own, can listen for the same [events](/rp-engine/api) and do
the same.
