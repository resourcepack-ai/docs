# Portals

An **addon** for [RP Engine](/rp-engine): a plugin that installs into the
engine and adds one thing, rather than a pack you author yourself. Drop it in
beside the engine and it brings its own content with it.

This one opens portals: a ring of sparks with a window in the middle onto
somewhere else. You see the far side through it as you walk up, with its own
blocks, sky, clouds and light, and you come out of the other ring still moving
the way you were. Right-click a portal to shut it down to a small glowing ball,
and again to open it.

## Installing

Portals needs **Paper 26.2** with players on **Java Edition 26.2** (the window
is drawn by the game's own shaders, through a resource pack, and that part is
written for 26.2), and **RP Engine 0.2.10** or newer, which draws the ring. No
client mods. Bedrock players do not see portals.

Drop `RPE-portals.jar` in `plugins/` beside `RPEngine.jar` and start the
server. Its content (the ring, its sounds and the shaders that draw the window)
installs itself into `plugins/RPEngine/content/portals/` on first boot, and the
engine reloads once to pick it up.

The window is drawn with the game's own block textures, which a server does not
have, so on first start the plugin downloads the Minecraft client jar for your
server's version from Mojang, once, into `plugins/RPEPortals/cache/`. Set
`textures.download: false` to skip that, and every block is drawn in a flat
colour instead.

## Placing portals

1. Stand where you want your portals to lead, facing the way you want to come
   out, and run `/portals mark`.
2. Go anywhere else, in the same world or another, and run `/portals place`. A
   pair opens: one ring a few blocks in front of you, facing you, and the other
   at your mark.
3. Walk into the ring. You come out of the other one.

`/portals place` again moves your pair: a player has one pair at a time
(`pairs-per-player` in the config), and placing another takes the oldest away.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/portals mark` | Your portals lead here, coming out facing the way you face |
| `/portals place [size]` | Open a pair in front of you, leading to your mark. A size (2 to 12 blocks across) needs `portals.place.size` |
| `/portals toggle` | Open or shut the portal you are looking at |
| `/portals remove [all]` | Take away the portal you are looking at, or all of yours |
| `/portals list` | Your pairs and where they lead |
| `/portals open <world> <x y z yaw> <x y z yaw> [size]` | A pair at set places, for staff and command blocks |
| `/portals closeall` | Take away every portal on the server |
| `/portals reload` | Re-read `config.yml` |

`/portal` works too. Right-click a portal to open or shut it, and sneak and
right-click it to take it away.

## Permissions

Every command has its own node, and so does every option that reaches past
your own portals.

| Node | What it allows | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `portals.mark` | `/portals mark` | Everyone |
| `portals.place` | `/portals place` | Everyone |
| `portals.place.size` | Choosing a ring's size | Operators |
| `portals.place.unlimited` | More pairs at once than `pairs-per-player` | Operators |
| `portals.place.otherworld` | A mark in another world | Everyone |
| `portals.toggle` | Opening and shutting your own portals | Everyone |
| `portals.toggle.others` | Opening and shutting anybody's | Operators |
| `portals.remove` | Taking away your own portals | Everyone |
| `portals.remove.others` | Taking away anybody's | Operators |
| `portals.list` | `/portals list` | Everyone |
| `portals.travel` | Walking through a portal | Everyone |
| `portals.open` | `/portals open` | Operators |
| `portals.closeall` | `/portals closeall` | Operators |
| `portals.reload` | `/portals reload` | Operators |
| `portals.debug` | `/portals debug` | Operators |

`portals.player` hands out everything a player needs and `portals.admin`
everything; nothing checks either directly. Negate `portals.travel` to make
portals something to look through only.

## Configuration

`plugins/RPEPortals/config.yml`:

```yaml
portal:
  # Diameter in blocks. The hole is 80% of it.
  size: 5.8
  # How far in front of you a portal opens, in blocks.
  distance: 3.5
  # Seconds a pair stays open before it closes itself. 0 keeps it open.
  lifetime: 0
  # How many pairs one player can have at once. Placing another takes the oldest away.
  pairs-per-player: 1

# Download the client jar from Mojang once, for the block textures in the window.
textures:
  download: true

sounds:
  open: portals:portal_open
  loop: portals:portal_loop
  close: portals:portal_close
  volume: 1.5
```

Every message the plugin sends is under `messages:` in the same file, in
MiniMessage.

## What you see through one

The window shows the blocks on the far side, ray-traced by the game's text
shader from maps the plugin sends: 128 blocks across, 63 deep and 28 tall in
full detail, with the block textures, plants, water, the biome's grass and leaf
colours, the light, and the game's own ambient occlusion. Beyond that the land
carries on for a few hundred blocks in coarser columns, treetops included, then
the sky, drawn with the game's own sky colours and the very clouds the far side
has, where it has them. Through a portal to the Nether you see its fog and its
light.

It shows **blocks only**: players, mobs and dropped items on the far side are
not in the window. What is drawn is read as the far side changes, a slice at a
time, so a block placed over there appears within a second or so.

Two limits worth knowing:

- **One mod of the text shader at a time.** The window is drawn by the game's
  text shader, which a resource pack can replace only once. Another plugin that
  replaces it too (RP Engine's own HUD shader objects, for one) and only one of
  them works.
- **It costs frame rate up close.** The window is a stack of maps in invisible
  item frames, each drawn by the game. A shut portal, or one seen from behind,
  draws nothing.

## For plugin developers

Portals has its own API: open pairs anywhere at any size, shut and open them,
and listen for every open, toggle, removal and crossing.

```java
PortalsApi api = PortalsApi.get();
api.open(
        PortalPlace.standing(spawnGround, 180f),
        PortalPlace.standing(arenaGround, 0f),
        PortalOptions.builder().size(9).lifetimeSeconds(60).build());
```

Add `depend: [RPEPortals]` (or `softdepend`) to your `plugin.yml` and compile
against the Portals jar. The events are `PortalOpenEvent`, `PortalToggleEvent`,
`PortalRemoveEvent` and `PortalTravelEvent`, all cancellable; the last can also
send the traveller somewhere else.
