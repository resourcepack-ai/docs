# Working furniture

A [placed model](/rp-engine/models) can do more than stand there. Five keys in
its `place:` block make it a working piece:

![Furniture models: a stool, a sofa, a desk setup and a drum kit](/images/furniture.webp)

```yaml
cabinet:
  material: PAPER
  model: cabinet
  place:
    storage: true        # a container. Right-click to open it
    jukebox: true        # plays music discs
    states: [...]        # looks a click cycles through: a lamp, a door, a TV
    connects: true       # joins its neighbours, like a sofa
    grow: { ... }        # turns into another piece over time
```

Whatever a piece holds (a container's contents, a disc, its current state, how
long it has been growing) is saved on the piece itself, in the world. It
survives a restart, moves with a copied region and goes back with a rolled-back
one.

## Storage

A cabinet, a wardrobe, a bin, a crate:

```yaml
cabinet:
  material: PAPER
  model: cabinet
  name: "&6Oak Cabinet"
  place:
    storage:
      type: chest        # chest | personal | enderchest | disposal | shulker
      rows: 3            # 1-6
      title: "Cabinet"   # & colour codes. Default: the item's name, else "Storage"
      open-sound: minecraft:block.chest.open
      close-sound: minecraft:block.chest.close
```

`storage: true` is a three-row chest, and `storage: personal` is that type with
everything else left at its default. Right-click to open it.

| Type | Whose contents | When it is broken |
|---|---|---|
| `chest` | The piece's. Everybody who opens it sees the same inventory, live | Spills on the ground |
| `personal` | Each player's own, shared by every piece of that kind: every wardrobe opens the same wardrobe for the same person, and nobody else sees in | Nothing to spill |
| `enderchest` | The player's own vanilla ender chest | Nothing |
| `disposal` | Nobody's. **What goes in is deleted when it closes** | Nothing |
| `shulker` | The piece's, like a chest | Gives back the item **with the contents inside it**. Place it again and they are back |

- Two players in one chest share one live inventory and see each other's moves,
  so items cannot be duplicated between them.
- Breaking a piece closes anyone's open screen first, then spills the
  contents. A break another plugin cancels spills nothing. A creative-mode
  break gives no piece back but still spills the contents, as a chest does.
- A `shulker` piece always gives back itself, so it ignores `drop:`. Empty, it
  is the same item as a new one and stacks with them.
- `personal` is keyed by the item's ID. Rename `mypack:wardrobe` and everybody's
  wardrobe starts empty; change the ID back and the old contents return.
- You can change `rows:` after people have filled things. A cabinet made
  smaller still opens big enough for what is in it, and shrinks once the extra
  rows are empty.

[Custom blocks](/rp-engine/blocks#what-else-a-block-does) take the same
`storage:` block.

## A jukebox

A gramophone, a radio, a boombox:

```yaml
gramophone:
  material: PAPER
  model: gramophone
  place:
    jukebox:
      volume: 1.0                       # 1 is a vanilla jukebox, heard ~64 blocks away. Up to 16
      pitch: 1.0                        # 0.5-2
      permission: mypack.jukebox.use    # leave out and anybody may
      playing-model: mypack:gramophone_on   # what it looks like while a disc is in it
```

`jukebox: true` is one with every default. Right-click it holding a music disc
to put the disc in (it leaves your hand unless you are in creative) and the
record plays at the piece. Right-click again and the disc pops out and the
music stops for everyone. Breaking the piece also gives the disc back.

Every vanilla disc works. From Minecraft 1.21, so does a disc a datapack adds,
but the server is not told which sound that disc plays, so it plays nothing
unless your server has a [sound](/rp-engine/sounds) with the song's own ID. The
console tells you once per song.

`playing-model` is an item ID (or a model ID with no item of its own) whose
model the piece wears while a disc is in it. It does nothing on an
[animated](/rp-engine/animation) piece.

<Callout type="note" title="Late arrivals hear nothing">
  The record is sent once, when the disc goes in, as with a vanilla jukebox.
  Somebody who walks up halfway through hears nothing until the next disc, and
  a restart does not resume it. The disc stays in the piece either way.
</Callout>

## States: lamps, doors and screens

A click can change a piece. Each entry under `states:` is one more look that a
right-click moves to, after the piece as you defined it. One entry is a switch;
three are a television with three channels. After the last state, the next
click goes back to the start.

```yaml
lamp:
  material: PAPER
  model: lamp
  place:
    light: 0                    # dark as placed
    states:
      - model: mypack:lamp_on   # an item whose model to wear. Leave out to keep it
        light: 15               # light in this state. Leave out for the piece's own
        sound: minecraft:block.lever.click   # played on arriving in this state

door:
  material: PAPER
  model: door
  place:
    solid: true
    base-sound: minecraft:block.wooden_door.close   # played on going back to the start
    reset-after: 5s             # shuts itself 5 seconds after the last click
    states:
      - turn: 90                # degrees added to the way it faces
        offset: [-0.4, 0, 0.4]  # blocks it moves: right, up, forward
        solid: false
        sound: minecraft:block.wooden_door.open
```

Anything a state leaves out stays as the piece was defined: a state that only
says `light: 15` keeps its model, its collision and its angle.

| Key | In a state |
|---|---|
| `model` | An item whose model it wears, or a model ID with no item of its own |
| `light` | 0-15. `0` is dark |
| `solid` | Whether a barrier stands in it, like the piece's own `solid:` |
| `turn` | Degrees added to the way it faces. A hinged door |
| `offset` | `[right, up, forward]` in blocks, the same directions as a [seat's](/rp-engine/placement-options#sitting) `x`, `y` and `z`. A sliding door |
| `sound` | Played on arriving in this state |

Beside `states:`:

- `reset-after:` takes the piece back to the start that long after the last
  click. Write `10t` for ticks, `10s` for seconds or `2m` for minutes; a bare
  number is ticks.
- `base-sound:` plays when it goes back to the start, by click or by timer.

A state's `model:` can name a model you never made an item for, such as
`mypack:lamp_on` with only `assets/models/lamp_on.bbmodel` in the pack. The same
goes for `playing-model` and the `connects` shapes below. If there is no item
or model by that name, the build says so.

A piece turns about its own centre and then moves, so a door hinged at one edge
is a quarter `turn` plus an `offset` that puts that edge back where it was. It
swings over a quarter of a second. Its hitbox, and what a vehicle drives into,
stay where it was placed.

The state is saved, so a lamp left on is still on after a restart. A piece left
open when its chunk unloaded starts its `reset-after` countdown again when the
chunk loads.

<Callout type="warning" title="Light and collision follow the piece's rules">
  A solid state gives no light. And the piece only ever changes a block it put
  there itself: if a player has put a block in the space a model stands in, the
  model leaves it alone and goes without being solid or lit in that state.
</Callout>

On an [animated](/rp-engine/animation) piece, `light`, `solid` and `sound`
work, but `model`, `turn` and `offset` do not, and the load says so. Give it a
right-click animation as well and a click does both: the state changes and the
animation plays.

## Joining up: sofas and counters

Pieces of the same kind put side by side can join into one run, the way a sofa
or a kitchen counter does:

```yaml
sofa:
  material: PAPER
  model: sofa
  place:
    connects: true      # or name any of them: { straight: ..., left: ..., right: ..., inner: ..., outer: ... }
```

| Look | Where the piece is |
|---|---|
| `straight` | The middle of a row, with one of its kind either side |
| `left` | The end with one only on its right |
| `right` | The other end, with one only on its left |
| `inner` | A corner its back touches |
| `outer` | A corner its front touches |

Each is an item (or a model) to wear. `connects: true` names them after the
piece: `sofa_straight`, `sofa_left` and so on. A piece standing alone wears its
own model. A joining piece always faces one of four ways (its `facing:` is
treated as `cardinal`) and joins only pieces of its own kind.

Corners follow the game's own rule for stairs, and a corner model is turned the
way the game turns corner stairs, so a corner drawn like vanilla's inner and
outer stairs comes out right. Placing or removing a piece updates the row
around it.

## Which click does what

A piece can be several of these at once, so a right-click goes to the first
that wants it:

1. The piece's own [`interact`](/rp-engine/actions#the-thing-in-the-world)
   actions run first, and a `cancel` in them stops the rest.
2. **Sneaking** on a piece that has a seat sits, and nothing below runs.
3. A **container** opens.
4. A **jukebox** takes a click that puts a disc in or takes one out.
5. **States** move to the next one, and a right-click animation plays with them.
6. A right-click animation plays.
7. Otherwise, a seat is sat on.

## Growing

A piece can turn into another piece over time: a sapling into a tree, a crop
through its stages.

```yaml
rose:
  material: PAPER
  model: rose
  place:
    grow:
      into: mypack:rose_stage2   # the item whose placed piece replaces this one
      after: 10s                 # the least time it stands first. t, s, m; bare = ticks
      chance: 0.5                # the chance per check once that has passed. Default 1
      light: 9                   # the least light at its block. Leave out for any

rose_stage2:
  material: PAPER
  model: rose2
  place:
    grow:
      into: mypack:rose_stage3
      after: 2m

rose_stage3:
  material: PAPER
  model: rose3
  place: {}                      # the last stage stays
```

Each piece is checked about once a second. Once it has stood for `after`, each
check has `chance` of growing it, so a field does not all change at once. The
new piece goes in the same block, facing the same way, and starts its own
clock.

`into` has to be an item with a `place:` block. If it is not, the load says so
and the piece never grows.

Growing is not breaking and placing: no break or place event fires, no
`remove` or `place` actions run, and the piece itself is not given back.
Anything it was holding (a disc, a container's contents) drops on the ground.

A piece is only checked while its chunk is loaded. The time keeps counting
while nobody is there, so a piece left long enough grows one stage at the first
check after somebody comes back. The clock is the game's tick count, so
`/time set` or a frozen daylight cycle does not stop growth.

[Custom blocks](/rp-engine/blocks#growing) grow differently, through stages of
one block. Use those for crops you want to farm in rows.

## From your own plugin

`ModelInfo` says what a piece's `place:` block declared: `storage()`,
`jukebox()`, `states()` and `grow()`. The engine handles every click itself;
cancelling `ModelInteractEvent` stops all of it. Growing fires neither
`ModelBreakEvent` nor `ModelPlaceEvent`. See [the plugin API](/rp-engine/api).
