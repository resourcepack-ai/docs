# Go Karts

An **addon** for [RP Engine](/rp-engine): a plugin that installs into the
engine and adds one thing, rather than a pack you author yourself. Drop it in
beside the engine and it brings its own content with it.

This one is go-kart racing. Up to eight karts line up on a grid, three red
lights go green, and you race three laps: drift through corners for a boost,
fly off ramps, take shortcuts, and drive through item boxes for bananas, nitro
and rockets. One command builds a whole circuit, and computer drivers can fill
the grid when you are short of players.

## Installing

It needs **Spigot or Paper, Java 21**, and **RP Engine 0.2.45 or newer**. On an
older engine Go Karts says so in the console and turns itself off. A few things
need newer versions:

| Needs | For |
|---|---|
| **Paper 1.21.4+** | Steering with A and D. On Spigot you steer by looking |
| **RP Engine 0.2.47+** | Karts, helmets and items drawn as icons in inventories (older engines show the shrunk 3D model) |
| **RP Engine 0.2.51+** | Bots, and front wheels and steering wheel that turn as you steer |
| **Minecraft 1.21.9+** | A visible driver in each bot's kart |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop it in">
    Put `RPE-gokarts.jar` in `plugins/` beside `RPEngine.jar` and start the
    server. Go Karts copies its karts, sounds, effects, HUD and results screen
    into `plugins/RPEngine/content/gokarts/` (RP Engine's content folder, which
    the engine builds its resource pack from) and reloads the engine once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a track">
    As an op, run `/kart track generate main`. It makes a new world for the
    track, builds the Sunny Meadows circuit there in a few seconds and takes
    you to it. Nothing in your existing worlds is touched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Race">
    `/kart join`, then `/kart start`. Alone is fine. Add `--withBots` to race
    computer drivers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you run Multiverse-Core, Go Karts loads after it, and it loads its own
track worlds again every time the server starts.

## Driving

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **W / S** | Throttle; brake, then reverse |
| **A / D** | Steer |
| **Space** | Handbrake. Held through a corner at speed it is a **drift**: blue sparks, then orange, then purple. Let go for a boost, bigger the longer you held it. In the air, a **trick**, and landing it is a boost |
| **F** or click | Use your item |
| **Q** | Throw away your item. With empty hands, go back to the last checkpoint if you are stuck or off the road |

**The start.** Three red lights a second apart, then green. Hold W too early
(when the second light comes on) and you stall. Press it as the lights go
green and you get a rocket start.

**The track.** Magenta pads on the straights are boost pads, and the lime pad
at the lip of a ramp is a jump. Grass and dirt slow you to walking pace, so
cutting a corner costs you. Laps count checkpoint by checkpoint, so skipping
part of the lap gets you nowhere. Every track has a shortcut (a tunnel, a
canyon floor, a frozen lake where the kart slides, a back alley), and either
way round counts.

Falling off, landing in water, or missing checkpoints for about twelve seconds
puts you back on the track. When the winner finishes, everybody else has
thirty seconds, then the results screen shows every driver's place, time and
best lap, and the track's lap record.

### Items

Drive through an item box to spin for an item. What you get depends on your
place: the leaders get things to defend with, the back of the field things to
catch up.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Nitro / Triple nitro | One boost, or three |
| Overdrive | Every press is a boost for six seconds |
| Banana / Banana bunch | Dropped behind you; whoever drives over it spins out. The bunch is three |
| Decoy box | Dropped behind you. Looks like an item box (but does not spin) and spins out whoever hits it |
| Bumper orb | Rolls ahead of you, bouncing off walls, and spins out whoever it meets, you included |
| Rocket | Flies at the kart one place ahead and throws it in the air |
| Storm drone | Flies to the leader and strikes everybody within four blocks of them |
| Hyper shield | Six seconds where nothing hits you, grass does not slow you, and anybody you touch spins out |
| Smoke bomb | Blinds everybody ahead of you for three seconds |
| Shockwave horn | Pushes back every kart within seven blocks and clears nearby bananas, decoys, orbs, rockets and a storm drone coming for you |
| Static surge | Everybody else loses their item and slows down for three seconds |

### What you see

The whole screen is one HUD: your item top left with a live minimap, lap,
clock and best lap top right, the standings down the right, and your speed and
place bottom right. The start lights and banners (final lap, wrong way,
finish) appear in the middle.

## Joining a race

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/kart join [track]` | Into the track's lobby, a glass box over the infield, until the race starts |
| `/kart colour <colour>` | The kart you would like: red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, pink or cyan. Everybody in a race gets a different one |
| `/kart leave` | Out of the race, with your inventory and position back. Always works, with no permission needed |
| `/kart results` | The last race's results |

`/kart` also answers to `/gokart`, `/karts` and `/gk`. During a race, players
can only run `/kart`, `/msg`, `/tell`, `/w`, `/r`, `/reply`, `/me` and `/list`
(change this with `allowed-commands`, or grant `gokarts.bypass.commands`).

## Running races

Races start when somebody with `gokarts.start` runs `/kart start [track]`. It
joins you first if you are not in. To have a lobby start by itself, set
`race.auto-start: true`: once `min-players` are waiting it counts down
`lobby-countdown` seconds and starts.

`/kart stop [track]` ends a race, or empties a lobby.

### Bots

`/kart start --withBots` fills the empty grid slots with computer drivers, and
`--withBots=3` adds three. They drive the same karts with the same controls:
they follow the road, drift for boosts, jump, use items and recover from
crashes. They appear on the minimap, in the standings and in the results, but
never in a track's records. Bots need RP Engine 0.2.51 or newer and the
`gokarts.start.bots` permission. Their names come from `bots.names` in the
config.

### Outside a race

`/kart give [colour] [player]` hands over a kart of your own. Right-click the
ground to park it, then right-click it to get in. Drifting, boost pads and a
speed readout above the hotbar work anywhere.

`/kart item <item> [player]` hands a racer an item as if from a box, for
testing or events. `/kart as <player> <subcommand>` runs a subcommand as
somebody else, checked against **their** permissions: an NPC that runs
`/kart as <player> join` for whoever clicks it is a join button.

## Building a track

### Generate one

```
/kart track generate <id> [prefab] [name]
```

Builds a complete circuit, with walls, kerbs, pads, item boxes, a start
gantry, a grandstand and the lobby, **in a new world of its own**
(`gokarts_<id>`). It replaces nothing. Generating the same id again makes a
fresh world and leaves the old one alone.

| Prefab | Track | Lap | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| `meadow` (default) | Sunny Meadows | 458 blocks | A jump over a river, a tunnel through a hill |
| `desert` | Dune Canyon | 528 blocks | A jump over a canyon, a lower road along its floor |
| `frost` | Frostbite Peak | 544 blocks | A figure of eight over a bridge, a frozen-lake shortcut |
| `neon` | Neon Nights | 742 blocks, two laps | Midnight city, an elevated road, a tunnel, a back alley |

For example, `/kart track generate night neon` builds Neon Nights as the
track `night`.

To build in an existing world instead:

```
/kart track generate <id> here confirm [prefab] [name]
```

The start line goes under your feet, with the straight running the way you
face.

<Callout type="warning" title="It replaces every block in a large box">
  Built `here`, a track replaces everything in a box up to about 200 by 230
  blocks, from fifteen below your feet to fourteen above. Without `confirm` it
  only tells you how big the box is.
</Callout>

From the console: `/kart track generate <id> at <world> <x> <y> <z> <yaw> [prefab] [name]`.

**`/kart track undo <id> confirm`** takes back the last generate of that
track. A track in its own world deletes the world; one built in place puts back
the blocks it replaced (but not the contents of chests or signs). It works
after a restart too.

### Set one up by hand

On a circuit you built yourself, stand in each place and run:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">`/kart track create <id>`</Step>
  <Step title="The lobby">`/kart track setlobby <id>` where racers wait.</Step>
  <Step title="The grid">
    `/kart track addgrid <id>` once per grid slot, facing the way the karts
    should face. `cleargrid` starts over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkpoints">
    `/kart track addcheckpoint <id> [radius]`: the start/finish line first,
    then round the lap in order, about every fifteen blocks. The radius
    defaults to 6 blocks. `clearcheckpoints` starts over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Item boxes">`/kart track addbox <id>` at each box. `clearboxes` removes them.</Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    `/kart track enable <id>`. If anything is missing it says what.
    `/kart track laps <id> <n>` sets this track's laps (0 uses the config's).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use magenta glazed terracotta for boost pads and lime glazed terracotta for
jump pads (both can be changed in the config). `/kart track list` shows every
track and what it still needs. `disable`, `tp`, `name` and
`delete <id> confirm` do what they say (deleting leaves the blocks).

## Permissions

One node per command. `gokarts.play` and `gokarts.admin` are shorthand that
grant the nodes under them.

| Node | What it allows | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `gokarts.play` | All four racer nodes below | Everyone |
| `gokarts.join` | `/kart join` | Everyone |
| `gokarts.colour` | `/kart colour` | Everyone |
| `gokarts.results` | `/kart results` | Everyone |
| `gokarts.ride` | Getting into a kart at all, a parked one included | Everyone |
| `gokarts.admin` | Every staff node below | Operators |
| `gokarts.start` | `/kart start` | Operators |
| `gokarts.start.bots` | `/kart start --withBots` | Operators |
| `gokarts.stop` | `/kart stop` | Operators |
| `gokarts.reload` | `/kart reload` | Operators |
| `gokarts.give` | `/kart give [colour]` | Operators |
| `gokarts.give.others` | `/kart give <colour> <player>` | Operators |
| `gokarts.item` | `/kart item` | Operators |
| `gokarts.as` | `/kart as` | Operators |
| `gokarts.track` | Track setup: `list`, `create`, `setlobby`, `addgrid`, `addcheckpoint`, `addbox`, `laps`, `enable`, `disable`, `tp`, `name` and the `clear` commands | Operators |
| `gokarts.track.generate` | `/kart track generate` | Operators |
| `gokarts.track.undo` | `/kart track undo` | Operators |
| `gokarts.track.delete` | `/kart track delete` | Operators |
| `gokarts.bypass.commands` | Any command during a race | Operators |

## Configuration

`plugins/RPEGoKarts/config.yml`. `/kart reload` rereads it, and `tracks.yml`
too unless a race is using it. An update adds new options with their defaults
and leaves yours alone. The main settings:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `race.laps` | `3` | Laps per race |
| `race.auto-start` | `false` | Start a lobby on its own once `min-players` (2) are waiting, after `lobby-countdown` (20) seconds |
| `race.finish-timeout` | `30` | Seconds the others have once the winner is home |
| `race.max-seconds` | `600` | The longest a race can run |
| `race.stuck-seconds` | `12` | Seconds without a checkpoint before you are put back |
| `karts.cruise-speed` | `13` | Top speed without a boost. Raise it to make karts faster |
| `karts.offroad-speed` | `6` | Speed on the `offroad-blocks` (grass, dirt, sand...) |
| `karts.boost-pad-block` / `jump-pad-block` | magenta / lime glazed terracotta | Which blocks are pads |
| `karts.ice-blocks` | ice, packed ice, blue ice | Blocks the kart slides on |
| `boost.*`, `drift.*` | | How strong and long each boost and drift level is |
| `items.enabled` | `true` | Item boxes on or off |
| `items.odds` | | How likely each item is, one row per place band from the front to the back |
| `bots.names` | | Names given to bots |
| `allowed-commands` | `msg, tell, w, r, reply, me, list` | Commands racers may use besides `/kart` |
| `ui.hud`, `ui.results` | `gokarts:hud`, `gokarts:results` | Point at your own RP Engine HUD or Dialog to replace them. It has to show the same values |

A kart's top speed with a boost is the vehicle's `speed:` in
`plugins/RPEngine/content/gokarts/vehicles/karts.yml`.

**Every word a player reads** is under `messages:` in the same file, in
[MiniMessage](https://docs.advntr.dev/minimessage/format.html).
