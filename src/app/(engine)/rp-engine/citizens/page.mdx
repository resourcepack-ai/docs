# Citizens

With Citizens installed, an NPC can wear one of your
[models](/rp-engine/models) and keep it through a chunk unloading, a respawn,
`/npc reload` and a server restart.

Look at the NPC and run:

```
/rp bind mypack:shopkeeper
```

There is no trait to add by hand and nothing to configure. `/rp unbind`, looking
at the NPC, takes the model off for good.

<Callout type="note" title="Player-shaped NPCs need the .others node">
  Most Citizens NPCs are player entities, so binding one counts as putting a
  model on another player and needs `rpengine.bind.others` (and
  `rpengine.unbind.others` to take it off) as well as `rpengine.bind`. Ops have
  both. See [Permissions](/rp-engine/permissions#pointing-a-command-at-somebody-else).
</Callout>

## How it stays on

Citizens removes an NPC's entity and makes a new one whenever its chunk
unloads, on `/npc reload` and on restart. Binding an NPC also adds an
`rpmodel` trait to it holding the model's ID. Citizens saves the trait with the
NPC, and RP Engine puts the model back on each time the NPC spawns.

## What the NPC keeps

Everything: its skin, name, pathing, commands, traits and identity. RP Engine
hides the vanilla body and attaches the model; take the model off and the NPC
is exactly what it was.

## Animating one

From your own plugin, through the [API](/rp-engine/api):

```java
models.animate(npc.getEntity(), "wave");
```

Citizens has no skill system to trigger animations from, so deciding when an
NPC waves is up to your plugin.
