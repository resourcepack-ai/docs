# Moving from Oraxen

RP Engine reads Oraxen's current item YAML directly. Copy the `.yml` files from
`plugins/Oraxen/items/<your-pack>/` into one RP Engine content pack (the normal
`items/` tree is accepted too), then copy
the **contents** of that pack's `assets/<your-pack>/` folder into `assets/`.

```
plugins/RPEngine/content/
  my_pack/
    items/                    ← from Oraxen/items/<your-pack>/
      weapons.yml
      furniture/chairs.yml
    assets/                   ← contents of Oraxen's assets/<your-pack>/
      models/item/
      textures/item/
```

No export or rewrite step is needed, and a `pack.yml` is optional. The loader
recognises the capital `Pack:` block in Oraxen item YAML and turns each item
into the same registry entry a hand-authored RP Engine item would create.

## What comes across

| Oraxen | RP Engine |
| --- | --- |
| item id | an [item](/rp-engine/items) under the content folder's namespace |
| `material` | `material` |
| `itemname`, `displayname` or `display_name` | `name` |
| `Pack.model` | `model` |
| `Pack.texture` | `texture` |
| `lore` | `lore` |
| `AttributeModifiers` | `attributes` (attribute, amount, operation and slot) |

## Furniture and blocks

Oraxen furniture becomes an RP Engine [`place:`](/rp-engine/models) item. A
barrier hitbox becomes `solid: true`, a seat becomes `seat:`, and an Oraxen
item drop becomes `drop:`. Display transforms, billboarding and culling are
renderer-specific settings and do not transfer.

`Mechanics.block` becomes an RP Engine [custom block](/rp-engine/blocks), not
a second item with the same ID. Its model, mushroom/note-block base, hardness,
preferred tool and placement sound transfer. RP Engine allocates its own block
state and keeps that allocation stable.

The art is copied to the regular RP Engine `assets/` folder. RP Engine then
generates the item-model wiring itself, so Oraxen CustomModelData numbers do
not need to match and cannot collide.

## Recipes and glyphs

Copy Oraxen's `recipes/` and `glyphs/` folders into the matching RP Engine
content pack. Recipe files named `shaped`, `shapeless`, `furnace`, `blasting`,
`smoking`, `campfire` or `stonecutting` translate directly, including vanilla
ingredients and Oraxen item results/ingredients. Static bitmap glyphs become
[icons](/rp-engine/icons), retaining their texture, height and ascent.

Animated GIF glyphs and multi-bitmap glyph grids report a clear warning rather
than producing a broken icon: they need generated frames or several allocated
characters, neither of which is a single RP Engine icon.

## What does not

The remaining Oraxen `Mechanics` are intentionally reported, not guessed at.
The item or block remains usable, while the startup report names each mechanic
that needs re-authoring, including click/script behaviour through
[actions](/rp-engine/actions).

HUDs, storage containers, connected furniture, beds/doors and text entities
are separate or stateful formats and are not imported yet.

<Callout type="warning" title="Placed Oraxen blocks are not RP Engine blocks">
  Oraxen's world state allocation belongs to Oraxen. A content migration gives
  you its item definitions and assets; existing placed blocks need rebuilding
  or a dedicated world migration.
</Callout>
