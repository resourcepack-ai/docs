# Seats, light and surfaces

Four more things a [`place:`](/rp-engine/models) block can say.

```yaml
chair:
  material: PAPER
  model: chair
  place:
    seat: 0.5            # sit on it, this far above its base. 0 is no seat
    light: 0             # 0-15, what it gives off
    surface: floor       # floor | wall | ceiling | any
    drop: mypack:shard   # what breaking it gives back. Default: itself
```

## Sitting

`seat:` is the height of the seat's surface above the model's base, in blocks.
A dining chair is about `0.5`, a bar stool nearer `1.1`, a log on the ground
`0.25`.

A seat is not always in the middle of a piece (a bench, an L-shaped sofa), so it
can be three numbers instead of one:

```yaml
place:
  seat:
    x: 0.4      # to the piece's right. Negative is left
    y: 0.5      # up, the same number the short form gives
    z: -0.15    # in front of it. Negative is behind
```

These are relative to the piece, not world x and z. They turn with it, so a
bench placed facing east seats people along itself rather than across it. Each
offset can be up to 16 blocks.

Right-click to sit, sneak to get up. One player per model, and a model with a
seat keeps its hitbox.

If every chair on your server sits people a little high or low, nudge them all
at once with `models.seat-offset` in [`config.yml`](/rp-engine/config)
(positive is higher), then `/rp reload`. No restart needed.

The seat is an invisible marker the player rides. It is never saved: it is
removed when the player gets up or leaves, when the model is broken, and when
the server stops, so nothing is left behind in the world.

What else a click does comes first in some cases. A model with a right-click
animation, [states](/rp-engine/furniture#states-lamps-doors-and-screens) or
[storage](/rp-engine/furniture#storage) does that on a plain click, and you
sit by sneaking and right-clicking. See
[which click does what](/rp-engine/furniture#which-click-does-what).

## Light

A display entity gives off no light, so `light:` puts a real light block in the
model's block space and removes it when the piece is broken. A lamp wants about
`14`.

<Callout type="warning" title="A solid piece cannot also be a lamp">
  The barrier and the light would have to be the same block, and the barrier
  wins. Put a non-solid lamp beside a solid model instead.
</Callout>

## Where it may go

`surface:` refuses a placement on the wrong face rather than turning the piece
sideways.

| `surface:` | Where it can go |
|---|---|
| `floor` | The top of a block. Chairs, tables, statues. The default |
| `wall` | The side of one. Torches, signs, brackets |
| `ceiling` | The underside. Chandeliers, hanging plants |
| `any` | Anywhere |

## What it drops

Breaking a piece gives back the item it was placed from, with whatever name or
enchantments that item had, so a renamed chair comes back renamed.

`drop:` gives a different content ID instead, for a piece meant to break rather
than come back: a statue that drops rubble, a window that drops shards. A
[`shulker` container](/rp-engine/furniture#storage) ignores `drop:`, because it
always gives back itself with its contents inside.
